San Francisco to Los Angeles by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 8 h 05 min to 12 h 15 min for the 559 km between San Francisco and Los Angeles. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

San Francisco and Los Angeles may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Los Angeles.

Tips for San Francisco to Los Angeles bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Francisco and Los Angeles. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
