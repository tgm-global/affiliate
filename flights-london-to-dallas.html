London to Dallas at a glance

The straight-line distance between London and Dallas is about 4,748 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 9 h 35 min to 11 h 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

Arriving in Dallas

Dallas is served by Dallas Fort Worth (DFW) and Love Field (DAL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking London to Dallas flights

  • Check US entry requirements well before you travel. Depending on your nationality you may need an ESTA or a visa.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Westbound daytime flights often suit a first day of sightseeing, while overnight flights suit people who can sleep on board.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in London or Dallas.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Dallas, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around London and Dallas, so check each before you decide.
