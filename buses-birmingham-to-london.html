Birmingham to London by coach at a glance

By road, Birmingham to London is about 101 miles, and we estimate 2 h 35 min to 4 h 10 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Birmingham and London may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in London.

Tips for Birmingham to London coach tickets

  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Birmingham and London. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
