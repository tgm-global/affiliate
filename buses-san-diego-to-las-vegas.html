San Diego to Las Vegas by bus at a glance

By road, San Diego to Las Vegas is about 265 miles, and we estimate 6 h 15 min to 9 h 25 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

San Diego and Las Vegas may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Las Vegas.

Tips for San Diego to Las Vegas bus tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Diego and Las Vegas. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
