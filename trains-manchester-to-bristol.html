Manchester to Bristol by train at a glance

A train from Manchester to Bristol typically takes 1 h 50 min to 3 h 20 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Manchester: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Bristol: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Bristol.

Tips for Manchester to Bristol train tickets

  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Bristol. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
