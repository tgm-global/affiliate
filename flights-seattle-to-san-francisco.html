Seattle to San Francisco at a glance

The straight-line distance between Seattle and San Francisco is about 680 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 45 min to 2 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Seattle

Seattle is served by Seattle-Tacoma (SEA).

Arriving in San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Seattle to San Francisco flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Seattle or San Francisco.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in San Francisco, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
