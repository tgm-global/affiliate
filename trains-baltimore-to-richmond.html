Baltimore to Richmond by train at a glance

For the roughly 207 km between Baltimore and Richmond, expect a journey of 2 h 40 min to 4 h 30 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Tips for Baltimore to Richmond train tickets

  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.

  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Richmond and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
