Chicago to Los Angeles at a glance

The straight-line distance between Chicago and Los Angeles is about 1,742 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 45 min to 4 h 40 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

Arriving in Los Angeles

Los Angeles is served by Los Angeles (LAX), Burbank (BUR) and Long Beach (LGB).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Chicago to Los Angeles flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Chicago or Los Angeles.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
