Houston to Miami at a glance

The straight-line distance between Houston and Miami is about 967 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 15 min to 2 h 50 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Houston

Houston is served by George Bush Intercontinental (IAH) and Hobby (HOU).

Arriving in Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Houston to Miami flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Houston or Miami.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
