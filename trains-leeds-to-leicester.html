Leeds to Leicester by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from Leeds to Leicester is about 82 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 1 h 05 min to 2 h for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Leeds: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Leicester: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Leicester.

Tips for Leeds to Leicester train tickets

  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Leicester. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
