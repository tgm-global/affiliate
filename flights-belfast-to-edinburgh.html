Belfast to Edinburgh at a glance

The straight-line distance between Belfast and Edinburgh is about 143 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 45 min to 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Belfast

Belfast is served by Belfast International (BFS) and George Best Belfast City (BHD).

Arriving in Edinburgh

Edinburgh is served by Edinburgh (EDI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Belfast to Edinburgh flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Belfast or Edinburgh.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Belfast and Edinburgh, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
