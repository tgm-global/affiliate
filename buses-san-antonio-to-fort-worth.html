San Antonio to Fort Worth by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 5 h 40 min to 8 h 40 min for the 387 km between San Antonio and Fort Worth. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

San Antonio and Fort Worth may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Fort Worth.

Tips for San Antonio to Fort Worth bus tickets

  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Antonio and Fort Worth. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
