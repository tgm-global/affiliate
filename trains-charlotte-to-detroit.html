Charlotte to Detroit by train at a glance

A train from Charlotte to Detroit typically takes 9 h 30 min to 16 h 10 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. Tips for Charlotte to Detroit train tickets

  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Detroit and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
