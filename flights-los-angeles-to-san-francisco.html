Los Angeles to San Francisco at a glance

On the map, Los Angeles and San Francisco are roughly 559 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 10 min to 1 h 25 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Los Angeles

Los Angeles is served by Los Angeles (LAX), Burbank (BUR) and Long Beach (LGB).

Arriving in San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Los Angeles to San Francisco flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in San Francisco, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
