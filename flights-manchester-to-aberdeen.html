Manchester to Aberdeen at a glance

The straight-line distance between Manchester and Aberdeen is about 254 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h to 1 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Manchester

Manchester is served by Manchester (MAN).

Arriving in Aberdeen

Aberdeen is served by Aberdeen (ABZ).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Manchester to Aberdeen flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Aberdeen, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Manchester or Aberdeen.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
