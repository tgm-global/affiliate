Detroit to New York by train at a glance

A train from Detroit to New York typically takes 9 h 05 min to 15 h 25 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Detroit: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. New York: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in New York.

Tips for Detroit to New York train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in New York and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
