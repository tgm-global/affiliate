Wolverhampton to Hull by coach at a glance

By road, Wolverhampton to Hull is about 109 miles, and we estimate 2 h 45 min to 4 h 25 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Wolverhampton and Hull may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Hull.

Tips for Wolverhampton to Hull coach tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check where coaches stop in Wolverhampton and Hull. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
