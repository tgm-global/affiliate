Los Angeles to Las Vegas by bus at a glance

A bus from Los Angeles to Las Vegas typically takes 5 h 25 min to 8 h 15 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Los Angeles and Las Vegas may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Las Vegas.

Tips for Los Angeles to Las Vegas bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
