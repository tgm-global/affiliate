London to Glasgow by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from London to Glasgow is about 344 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 4 h 20 min to 7 h 55 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

London: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Glasgow: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Glasgow.

Tips for London to Glasgow train tickets

  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Glasgow. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
