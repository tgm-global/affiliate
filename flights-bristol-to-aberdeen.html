Bristol to Aberdeen at a glance

The straight-line distance between Bristol and Aberdeen is about 394 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 15 min to 1 h 30 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Bristol to Aberdeen flights

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Bristol and Aberdeen, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
