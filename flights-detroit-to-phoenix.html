Detroit to Phoenix at a glance

The straight-line distance between Detroit and Phoenix is about 1,687 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 40 min to 4 h 30 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Detroit and Phoenix, so check each before you decide.
