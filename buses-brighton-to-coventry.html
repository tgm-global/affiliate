Brighton to Coventry by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 3 h 10 min to 5 h for the 200 km between Brighton and Coventry. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Brighton and Coventry may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Coventry.

Tips for Brighton to Coventry coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Brighton and Coventry. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
