Edinburgh to Leeds at a glance

Expect a flight of 50 min to 1 h for the 261 km between Edinburgh and Leeds. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Edinburgh

Edinburgh is served by Edinburgh (EDI).

Arriving in Leeds

Leeds is served by Leeds Bradford (LBA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Edinburgh to Leeds flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Edinburgh and Leeds, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Edinburgh or Leeds.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.

Other ways to travel
