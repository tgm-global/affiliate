Stoke-on-Trent to Glasgow by coach at a glance

By road, Stoke-on-Trent to Glasgow is about 214 miles, and we estimate 5 h 15 min to 8 h 15 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Stoke-on-Trent and Glasgow may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Glasgow.

Tips for Stoke-on-Trent to Glasgow coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Stoke-on-Trent and Glasgow. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
