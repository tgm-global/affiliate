Types of tours to look for in Phoenix

Evening and night tours

Evening walks and transport-based sightseeing can suit travellers who prefer to avoid daytime heat or crowds.

Private guides

A private guide can adapt the pace and route for families, accessibility needs or special interests.

Day trips to nearby places

Coach or minibus excursions let you see places outside the centre without arranging your own transport.

Museum and heritage visits

Timed-entry tickets and guided visits can save queueing at popular attractions.

Hop-on hop-off sightseeing

Where offered, multi-stop routes help you cover the main sights at your own pace.

Further afield

Phoenix is fairly remote. The closest place in our guide is Tucson, about 171 km away, so plan extra travel time for any excursion.

Tips for booking tours in Phoenix

  • Read the cancellation policy. Flexible bookings help if the weather or your plans change.
  • Check the language, group size and total duration of the tour.
  • Book earlier for peak season, weekends and major events.
  • Check accessibility information if anyone in your group has mobility needs.
  • Check the meeting point and start time before you book, and arrive early.
