Philadelphia to Detroit by bus at a glance

By road, Philadelphia to Detroit is about 442 miles, and we estimate 10 h 10 min to 15 h 20 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Philadelphia and Detroit may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Detroit.

Tips for Philadelphia to Detroit bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Philadelphia and Detroit. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
