Washington, DC to Edinburgh at a glance

Expect a flight of 6 h 20 min to 7 h 55 min for the 5,569 km between Washington, DC and Edinburgh. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

Arriving in Edinburgh

Edinburgh is served by Edinburgh (EDI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Washington, DC to Edinburgh flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Edinburgh, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Washington, DC and Edinburgh, so check each before you decide.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
