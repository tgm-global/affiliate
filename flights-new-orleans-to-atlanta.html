New Orleans to Atlanta at a glance

On the map, New Orleans and Atlanta are roughly 682 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 15 min to 1 h 35 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Tips for booking New Orleans to Atlanta flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around New Orleans and Atlanta, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in New Orleans or Atlanta.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Atlanta, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
