Chicago to Tampa at a glance

The straight-line distance between Chicago and Tampa is about 1,006 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 20 min to 2 h 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Arriving in Tampa

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Chicago to Tampa flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Tampa, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Chicago and Tampa, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Chicago or Tampa.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
