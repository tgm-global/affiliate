Boston to Glasgow at a glance

The straight-line distance between Boston and Glasgow is about 3,031 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 5 h 40 min to 7 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Boston

Boston is served by Logan (BOS).

Arriving in Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Boston to Glasgow flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Boston and Glasgow, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Boston or Glasgow.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
