Dallas to Manchester at a glance

The straight-line distance between Dallas and Manchester is about 4,609 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 8 h 20 min to 10 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Dallas

Dallas is served by Dallas Fort Worth (DFW) and Love Field (DAL).

Arriving in Manchester

Manchester is served by Manchester (MAN).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Dallas to Manchester flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Dallas and Manchester, so check each before you decide.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Dallas or Manchester.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
