Washington, DC to Seattle at a glance

The straight-line distance between Washington, DC and Seattle is about 2,321 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 4 h 50 min to 6 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

Arriving in Seattle

Seattle is served by Seattle-Tacoma (SEA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Washington, DC to Seattle flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Washington, DC or Seattle.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
