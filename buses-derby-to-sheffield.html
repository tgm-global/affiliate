Derby to Sheffield by coach at a glance

By road, Derby to Sheffield is about 32 miles, and we estimate 55 min to 1 h 40 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Derby and Sheffield may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Sheffield.

Tips for Derby to Sheffield coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check where coaches stop in Derby and Sheffield. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
