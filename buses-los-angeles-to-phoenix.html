Los Angeles to Phoenix by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 8 h 15 min to 12 h 30 min for the 574 km between Los Angeles and Phoenix. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Los Angeles and Phoenix may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Phoenix.

Tips for Los Angeles to Phoenix bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Los Angeles and Phoenix. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
