Leeds to London at a glance

The straight-line distance between Leeds and London is about 169 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 50 min to 1 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Leeds

Leeds is served by Leeds Bradford (LBA).

Arriving in London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Leeds to London flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Leeds and London, so check each before you decide.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Leeds to London or a coach from Leeds to London. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
