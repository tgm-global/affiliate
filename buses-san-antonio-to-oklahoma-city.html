San Antonio to Oklahoma City by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 9 h 45 min to 14 h 40 min for the 678 km between San Antonio and Oklahoma City. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

San Antonio and Oklahoma City may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Oklahoma City.

Tips for San Antonio to Oklahoma City bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Antonio and Oklahoma City. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
