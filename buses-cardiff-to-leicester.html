Cardiff to Leicester by coach at a glance

By road, Cardiff to Leicester is about 118 miles, and we estimate 3 h to 4 h 45 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Cardiff and Leicester may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Leicester.

Tips for Cardiff to Leicester coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
