Edinburgh to London at a glance

The straight-line distance between Edinburgh and London is about 331 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 05 min to 1 h 25 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Edinburgh

Edinburgh is served by Edinburgh (EDI).

Arriving in London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Edinburgh to London flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Edinburgh and London, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Edinburgh or London.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in London, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Edinburgh to London or a coach from Edinburgh to London. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
