Glasgow to Plymouth by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from Glasgow to Plymouth is about 379 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 4 h 45 min to 8 h 40 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Glasgow: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Plymouth: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Plymouth.

Tips for Glasgow to Plymouth train tickets

  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Plymouth. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
