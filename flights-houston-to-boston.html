Houston to Boston at a glance

The straight-line distance between Houston and Boston is about 1,604 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 30 min to 4 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Houston

Houston is served by George Bush Intercontinental (IAH) and Hobby (HOU).

Arriving in Boston

Boston is served by Logan (BOS).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Houston to Boston flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Boston, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
