Liverpool to Belfast at a glance

Expect a flight of 45 min to 55 min for the 233 km between Liverpool and Belfast. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Liverpool

Liverpool is served by Liverpool John Lennon (LPL).

Arriving in Belfast

Belfast is served by Belfast International (BFS) and George Best Belfast City (BHD).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Liverpool to Belfast flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Belfast, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Liverpool and Belfast, so check each before you decide.
