Philadelphia to Washington, DC by train at a glance

For the roughly 197 km between Philadelphia and Washington, DC, expect a journey of 1 h 50 min to 3 h 05 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

Philadelphia: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Washington, DC: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Washington, DC.

Tips for Philadelphia to Washington, DC train tickets

  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Washington, DC and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
