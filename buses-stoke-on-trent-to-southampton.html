Stoke-on-Trent to Southampton by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 3 h 45 min to 5 h 55 min for the 239 km between Stoke-on-Trent and Southampton. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Stoke-on-Trent and Southampton may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Southampton.

Tips for Stoke-on-Trent to Southampton coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Check where coaches stop in Stoke-on-Trent and Southampton. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
