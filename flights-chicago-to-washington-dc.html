Chicago to Washington, DC at a glance

The straight-line distance between Chicago and Washington, DC is about 593 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 35 min to 2 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

Arriving in Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Chicago to Washington, DC flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Washington, DC, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
