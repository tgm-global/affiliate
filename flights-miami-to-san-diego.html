Miami to San Diego at a glance

Expect a flight of 4 h 40 min to 5 h 50 min for the 3,650 km between Miami and San Diego. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

Arriving in San Diego

San Diego is served by San Diego (SAN).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Miami to San Diego flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in San Diego, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Miami or San Diego.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Miami and San Diego, so check each before you decide.
