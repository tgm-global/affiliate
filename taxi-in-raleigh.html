Airport transfers for Raleigh

Raleigh is served by Raleigh-Durham (RDU). Transfer time and cost depend on the airport you use, the time of day and traffic, so check the details on your booking.

Pre-booked or on the day?

On-the-day taxis and rideshares are convenient for short trips. Pre-booking gives you a confirmed vehicle and a quoted price before you travel, which many people prefer after a long flight. Compare both and choose what suits your arrival time.

Tips for taxi and transfer bookings in Raleigh

  • Use licensed operators only, and check that the vehicle and driver match your booking.
  • Check where the meeting point is. Airports often have a set pick-up area.
  • Ask for a child seat when you book if you need one.
  • Confirm whether the price is fixed or metered before you set off.
  • Check luggage space if you are travelling in a group.

Frequently asked questions

Should I pre-book a taxi in Raleigh?

Pre-booking is helpful if you arrive late, travel with children or have a lot of luggage. For short daytime trips, an on-the-day taxi may be enough.

How do I get from the airport to the centre of Raleigh?

Raleigh is served by Raleigh-Durham (RDU). Options usually include taxis, transfers and public transport. Check which suits your arrival time.

Are fares fixed?

Pre-booked transfers are usually quoted in advance, while metered taxis depend on distance and time. Confirm before you travel.
