New York to Phoenix at a glance

The straight-line distance between New York and Phoenix is about 2,139 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 4 h 30 min to 5 h 35 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

Arriving in Phoenix

Phoenix is served by Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to Phoenix flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around New York and Phoenix, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Phoenix, especially if you arrive late at night.
