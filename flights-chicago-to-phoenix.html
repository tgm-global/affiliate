Chicago to Phoenix at a glance

The straight-line distance between Chicago and Phoenix is about 1,452 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 10 min to 4 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

Arriving in Phoenix

Phoenix is served by Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Chicago to Phoenix flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Chicago or Phoenix.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Chicago and Phoenix, so check each before you decide.
