Philadelphia to San Francisco at a glance

The straight-line distance between Philadelphia and San Francisco is about 2,517 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 5 h 10 min to 6 h 25 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Philadelphia

Philadelphia is served by Philadelphia (PHL).

Arriving in San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Philadelphia to San Francisco flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Philadelphia and San Francisco, so check each before you decide.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in San Francisco, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Philadelphia or San Francisco.
