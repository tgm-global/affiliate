Edinburgh to Liverpool at a glance

On the map, Edinburgh and Liverpool are roughly 282 km apart. A typical flying time is 50 min to 1 h, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Edinburgh to Liverpool flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Liverpool, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Edinburgh and Liverpool, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
