Phoenix to Las Vegas by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 6 h to 9 h 10 min for the 412 km between Phoenix and Las Vegas. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Phoenix and Las Vegas may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Las Vegas.

Tips for Phoenix to Las Vegas bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
