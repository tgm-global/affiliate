New York to San Francisco at a glance

The straight-line distance between New York and San Francisco is about 2,566 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 5 h 15 min to 6 h 35 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

Arriving in San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to San Francisco flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
