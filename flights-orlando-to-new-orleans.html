Orlando to New Orleans at a glance

The straight-line distance between Orlando and New Orleans is about 533 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 30 min to 1 h 50 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Orlando to New Orleans flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Orlando and New Orleans, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
