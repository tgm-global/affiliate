Edinburgh to Los Angeles at a glance

The straight-line distance between Edinburgh and Los Angeles is about 5,146 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 10 h 20 min to 12 h 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Edinburgh

Edinburgh is served by Edinburgh (EDI).

Arriving in Los Angeles

Los Angeles is served by Los Angeles (LAX), Burbank (BUR) and Long Beach (LGB).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Edinburgh to Los Angeles flights

  • Check US entry requirements well before you travel. Depending on your nationality you may need an ESTA or a visa.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Westbound daytime flights often suit a first day of sightseeing, while overnight flights suit people who can sleep on board.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Edinburgh or Los Angeles.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Los Angeles, especially if you arrive late at night.
