Leicester to Cardiff by train at a glance

For the roughly 190 km between Leicester and Cardiff, expect a journey of 1 h 35 min to 2 h 50 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Leicester: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Cardiff: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Cardiff.

Tips for Leicester to Cardiff train tickets

  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Cardiff. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
