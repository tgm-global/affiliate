Leicester to Liverpool by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 2 h 25 min to 3 h 55 min for the 151 km between Leicester and Liverpool. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Leicester and Liverpool may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Liverpool.

Tips for Leicester to Liverpool coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Leicester and Liverpool. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
