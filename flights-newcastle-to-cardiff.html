Newcastle to Cardiff at a glance

The straight-line distance between Newcastle and Cardiff is about 250 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h to 1 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Newcastle to Cardiff flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Newcastle or Cardiff.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
