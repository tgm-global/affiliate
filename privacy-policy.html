Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use this website.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What we collect

This website has no accounts, no contact or query forms and no ordering system, so we do not ask you to enter personal details. Route and place search boxes work in your browser to take you to a page or a partner link.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on a static hosting service. Hosting providers may record technical data such as IP address, browser type and time of visit in server logs for security and operation.

Partner websites

When you click a booking button you leave this site. The partner may use cookies and collect data under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to read it before you book.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies itself. See our Cookie Policy. If analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, for example under UK GDPR or US state privacy laws, including the right to ask what data is held, to correct or delete it, or to object to processing. Because we do not collect personal data through this website, requests normally relate to hosting logs or to partner sites.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
