Hartford to New York by train at a glance

For the roughly 161 km between Hartford and New York, expect a journey of 1 h 30 min to 2 h 40 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

Hartford: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. New York: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in New York.

Tips for Hartford to New York train tickets

  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in New York and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
