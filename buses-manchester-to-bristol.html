Manchester to Bristol by coach at a glance

By road, Manchester to Bristol is about 141 miles, and we estimate 3 h 30 min to 5 h 35 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Manchester and Bristol may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bristol.

Tips for Manchester to Bristol coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Check where coaches stop in Manchester and Bristol. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
