Fort Worth to Austin by bus at a glance

By road, Fort Worth to Austin is about 174 miles, and we estimate 4 h 10 min to 6 h 25 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Fort Worth and Austin may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Austin.

Tips for Fort Worth to Austin bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
