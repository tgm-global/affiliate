Boston to Philadelphia by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 6 h 20 min to 9 h 40 min for the 436 km between Boston and Philadelphia. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Boston and Philadelphia may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Philadelphia.

Tips for Boston to Philadelphia bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Boston and Philadelphia. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
