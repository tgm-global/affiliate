Philadelphia to Chicago at a glance

On the map, Philadelphia and Chicago are roughly 1,067 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 45 min to 2 h 10 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Tips for booking Philadelphia to Chicago flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Chicago, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Philadelphia and Chicago, so check each before you decide.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
