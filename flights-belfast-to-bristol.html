Belfast to Bristol at a glance

The straight-line distance between Belfast and Bristol is about 258 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h to 1 h 15 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
