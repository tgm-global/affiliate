London to Belfast at a glance

The straight-line distance between London and Belfast is about 322 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 05 min to 1 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

Arriving in Belfast

Belfast is served by Belfast International (BFS) and George Best Belfast City (BHD).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking London to Belfast flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Belfast, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in London or Belfast.
