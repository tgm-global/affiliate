Philadelphia to Columbus by bus at a glance

By road, Philadelphia to Columbus is about 415 miles, and we estimate 9 h 35 min to 14 h 25 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Philadelphia and Columbus may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Columbus.

Tips for Philadelphia to Columbus bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Philadelphia and Columbus. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
