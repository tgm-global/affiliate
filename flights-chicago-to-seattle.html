Chicago to Seattle at a glance

Expect a flight of 3 h 45 min to 4 h 35 min for the 2,788 km between Chicago and Seattle. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Arriving in Seattle

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Chicago to Seattle flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Chicago or Seattle.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Chicago and Seattle, so check each before you decide.
