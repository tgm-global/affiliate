Stops and arrival

Charlotte and Jacksonville may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Jacksonville.

Tips for Charlotte to Jacksonville bus tickets

  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Charlotte and Jacksonville. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
