San Diego to San Jose by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 9 h 35 min to 14 h 30 min for the 670 km between San Diego and San Jose. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

San Diego and San Jose may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in San Jose.

Tips for San Diego to San Jose bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Diego and San Jose. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
