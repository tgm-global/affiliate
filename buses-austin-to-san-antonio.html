Austin to San Antonio by bus at a glance

By road, Austin to San Antonio is about 74 miles, and we estimate 1 h 55 min to 3 h 05 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Austin and San Antonio may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in San Antonio.

Tips for Austin to San Antonio bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Austin and San Antonio. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
