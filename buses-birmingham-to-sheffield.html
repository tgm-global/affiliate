Birmingham to Sheffield by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 1 h 45 min to 2 h 50 min for the 104 km between Birmingham and Sheffield. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Birmingham and Sheffield may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Sheffield.

Tips for Birmingham to Sheffield coach tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check where coaches stop in Birmingham and Sheffield. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
