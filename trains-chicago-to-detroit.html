Chicago to Detroit by train at a glance

A train from Chicago to Detroit typically takes 4 h 40 min to 7 h 50 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Chicago: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Detroit: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Detroit.

Tips for Chicago to Detroit train tickets

  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Detroit and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
