Birmingham to Boston at a glance

The straight-line distance between Birmingham and Boston is about 3,179 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 6 h 35 min to 8 h 15 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Birmingham

Birmingham is served by Birmingham (BHX).

Arriving in Boston

Boston is served by Logan (BOS).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Birmingham to Boston flights

  • Check US entry requirements well before you travel. Depending on your nationality you may need an ESTA or a visa.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Westbound daytime flights often suit a first day of sightseeing, while overnight flights suit people who can sleep on board.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Birmingham and Boston, so check each before you decide.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Boston, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Birmingham or Boston.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
