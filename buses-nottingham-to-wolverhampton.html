Nottingham to Wolverhampton by coach at a glance

By road, Nottingham to Wolverhampton is about 48 miles, and we estimate 1 h 20 min to 2 h 15 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Nottingham and Wolverhampton may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Wolverhampton.

Tips for Nottingham to Wolverhampton coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check where coaches stop in Nottingham and Wolverhampton. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
