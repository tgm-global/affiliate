Miami to London at a glance

On the map, Miami and London are roughly 7,126 km apart. A typical flying time is 8 h to 9 h 55 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

Arriving in London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Miami to London flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in London, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Miami and London, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
