Aberdeen to London at a glance

Expect a flight of 1 h 15 min to 1 h 30 min for the 639 km between Aberdeen and London. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Aberdeen

Aberdeen is served by Aberdeen (ABZ).

Arriving in London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Aberdeen to London flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Aberdeen or London.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in London, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Aberdeen and London, so check each before you decide.
