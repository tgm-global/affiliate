Belfast to Liverpool at a glance

The straight-line distance between Belfast and Liverpool is about 145 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 45 min to 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Belfast

Belfast is served by Belfast International (BFS) and George Best Belfast City (BHD).

Arriving in Liverpool

Liverpool is served by Liverpool John Lennon (LPL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Belfast to Liverpool flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Liverpool, especially if you arrive late at night.
