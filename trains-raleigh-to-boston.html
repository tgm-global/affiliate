Raleigh to Boston by train at a glance

For the roughly 980 km between Raleigh and Boston, expect a journey of 11 h 25 min to 19 h 25 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Tips for Raleigh to Boston train tickets

  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Boston and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
