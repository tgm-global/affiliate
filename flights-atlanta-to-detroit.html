Atlanta to Detroit at a glance

The straight-line distance between Atlanta and Detroit is about 597 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 35 min to 2 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Atlanta is served by Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL).

Arriving in Detroit

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Atlanta to Detroit flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Atlanta and Detroit, so check each before you decide.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Atlanta or Detroit.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
