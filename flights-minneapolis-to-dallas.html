Minneapolis to Dallas at a glance

Expect a flight of 2 h 05 min to 2 h 35 min for the 1,390 km between Minneapolis and Dallas. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Minneapolis

Minneapolis is served by Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP).

Arriving in Dallas

Dallas is served by Dallas Fort Worth (DFW) and Love Field (DAL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Minneapolis to Dallas flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Minneapolis or Dallas.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
