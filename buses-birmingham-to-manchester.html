Birmingham to Manchester by coach at a glance

By road, Birmingham to Manchester is about 71 miles, and we estimate 1 h 50 min to 3 h 05 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Birmingham and Manchester may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Manchester.

Tips for Birmingham to Manchester coach tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
