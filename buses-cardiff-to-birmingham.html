Cardiff to Birmingham by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 2 h 15 min to 3 h 40 min for the 141 km between Cardiff and Birmingham. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Cardiff and Birmingham may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Birmingham.

Tips for Cardiff to Birmingham coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Cardiff and Birmingham. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
