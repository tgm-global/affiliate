Chicago to Detroit at a glance

The straight-line distance between Chicago and Detroit is about 237 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 55 min to 1 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

Arriving in Detroit

Detroit is served by Detroit Metropolitan (DTW).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Chicago to Detroit flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Detroit, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Chicago and Detroit, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
