Glasgow to New York at a glance

The straight-line distance between Glasgow and New York is about 3,220 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 6 h 40 min to 8 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

Arriving in New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Glasgow to New York flights

  • Check US entry requirements well before you travel. Depending on your nationality you may need an ESTA or a visa.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Westbound daytime flights often suit a first day of sightseeing, while overnight flights suit people who can sleep on board.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Glasgow and New York, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Glasgow or New York.
