New York to Tampa at a glance

The straight-line distance between New York and Tampa is about 1,004 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 20 min to 2 h 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Arriving in Tampa

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to Tampa flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around New York and Tampa, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Tampa, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
