Birmingham to Glasgow by coach at a glance

A coach from Birmingham to Glasgow typically takes 6 h 10 min to 9 h 40 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Birmingham and Glasgow may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Glasgow.

Tips for Birmingham to Glasgow coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Check where coaches stop in Birmingham and Glasgow. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
