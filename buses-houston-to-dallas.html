Houston to Dallas by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 5 h 20 min to 8 h 05 min for the 362 km between Houston and Dallas. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Houston and Dallas may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Dallas.

Tips for Houston to Dallas bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
