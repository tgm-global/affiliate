Bristol to Edinburgh at a glance

The straight-line distance between Bristol and Edinburgh is about 312 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 05 min to 1 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Bristol to Edinburgh flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Bristol or Edinburgh.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
