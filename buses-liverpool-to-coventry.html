Liverpool to Coventry by coach at a glance

A coach from Liverpool to Coventry typically takes 2 h 25 min to 3 h 50 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Liverpool and Coventry may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Coventry.

Tips for Liverpool to Coventry coach tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check where coaches stop in Liverpool and Coventry. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
