Indianapolis to Chicago by bus at a glance

By road, Indianapolis to Chicago is about 165 miles, and we estimate 4 h to 6 h 05 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Indianapolis and Chicago may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Chicago.

Tips for Indianapolis to Chicago bus tickets

  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
