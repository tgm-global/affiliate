Edinburgh to London by coach at a glance

By road, Edinburgh to London is about 331 miles, and we estimate 8 h to 12 h 30 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Edinburgh and London may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in London.

Tips for Edinburgh to London coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check where coaches stop in Edinburgh and London. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
