Manchester to Bradford by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 55 min to 1 h 35 min for the 47 km between Manchester and Bradford. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Manchester and Bradford may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bradford.

Tips for Manchester to Bradford coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Manchester and Bradford. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
