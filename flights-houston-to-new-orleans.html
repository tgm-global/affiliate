Houston to New Orleans at a glance

On the map, Houston and New Orleans are roughly 511 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 05 min to 1 h 20 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Arriving in New Orleans

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Houston to New Orleans flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Houston or New Orleans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in New Orleans, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
