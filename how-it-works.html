How It Works

Three simple steps from planning to booking.

1. Find your route

Use the search box on the home page or browse the flights, trains, buses, tours, holiday packages and taxi sections. Each route and place has its own page.

2. Read the planning notes

Every page shows distances, typical time ranges, airport or station notes and tips written for that place or route.

3. Check live options

Press a booking button to open our partner's website in a new tab and see current availability, prices and terms.

If we do not have a page

If you search for a route we do not have a guide for, the site opens the partner's search directly so you can still check options.

Good to know

You never enter personal details on this site. Your booking and payment happen on the partner's website.
