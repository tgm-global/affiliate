Leeds to Brighton by coach at a glance

A coach from Leeds to Brighton typically takes 5 h 15 min to 8 h 15 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Leeds and Brighton may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Brighton.

Tips for Leeds to Brighton coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Leeds and Brighton. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
