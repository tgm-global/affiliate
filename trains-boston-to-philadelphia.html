Boston to Philadelphia by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from Boston to Philadelphia is about 271 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 3 h 40 min to 6 h 15 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

Boston: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Philadelphia: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Philadelphia.

Tips for Boston to Philadelphia train tickets

  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Philadelphia and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
