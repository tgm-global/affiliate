Austin to Dallas at a glance

The straight-line distance between Austin and Dallas is about 182 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 50 min to 1 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Austin

Austin is served by Austin-Bergstrom (AUS).

Arriving in Dallas

Dallas is served by Dallas Fort Worth (DFW) and Love Field (DAL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Austin to Dallas flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Austin or Dallas.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Austin and Dallas, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a coach from Austin to Dallas. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
