London to Derby by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from London to Derby is about 113 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 1 h 30 min to 2 h 45 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

London: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Derby: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Derby.

Tips for London to Derby train tickets

  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Derby. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
