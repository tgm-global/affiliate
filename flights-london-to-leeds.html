London to Leeds at a glance

Expect a flight of 50 min to 1 h for the 272 km between London and Leeds. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

Arriving in Leeds

Leeds is served by Leeds Bradford (LBA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking London to Leeds flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in London or Leeds.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from London to Leeds or a coach from London to Leeds. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
