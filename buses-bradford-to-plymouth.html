Bradford to Plymouth by coach at a glance

By road, Bradford to Plymouth is about 258 miles, and we estimate 6 h 20 min to 9 h 50 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Bradford and Plymouth may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Plymouth.

Tips for Bradford to Plymouth coach tickets

  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
