San Jose to Los Angeles by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 7 h 10 min to 10 h 50 min for the 492 km between San Jose and Los Angeles. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

San Jose and Los Angeles may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Los Angeles.

Tips for San Jose to Los Angeles bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Jose and Los Angeles. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
