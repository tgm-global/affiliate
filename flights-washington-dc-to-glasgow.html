Washington, DC to Glasgow at a glance

On the map, Washington, DC and Glasgow are roughly 5,507 km apart. A typical flying time is 6 h 20 min to 7 h 50 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

Arriving in Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Washington, DC to Glasgow flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Washington, DC or Glasgow.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Glasgow, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
