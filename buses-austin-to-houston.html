Austin to Houston by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 3 h 35 min to 5 h 30 min for the 235 km between Austin and Houston. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Austin and Houston may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Houston.

Tips for Austin to Houston bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Austin and Houston. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
