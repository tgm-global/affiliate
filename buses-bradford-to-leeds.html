Bradford to Leeds by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 25 min to 50 min for the 13 km between Bradford and Leeds. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Bradford and Leeds may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Leeds.

Tips for Bradford to Leeds coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check where coaches stop in Bradford and Leeds. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
