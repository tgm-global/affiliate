Charlotte to Columbus by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 8 h 05 min to 12 h 15 min for the 559 km between Charlotte and Columbus. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Charlotte and Columbus may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Columbus.

Tips for Charlotte to Columbus bus tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
