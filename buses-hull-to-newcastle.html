Hull to Newcastle by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 2 h 35 min to 4 h 05 min for the 160 km between Hull and Newcastle. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Hull and Newcastle may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Newcastle.

Tips for Hull to Newcastle coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check where coaches stop in Hull and Newcastle. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
