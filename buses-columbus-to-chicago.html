Columbus to Chicago by bus at a glance

By road, Columbus to Chicago is about 276 miles, and we estimate 6 h 30 min to 9 h 50 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Columbus and Chicago may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Chicago.

Tips for Columbus to Chicago bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Columbus and Chicago. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
