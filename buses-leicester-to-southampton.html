Leicester to Southampton by coach at a glance

By road, Leicester to Southampton is about 121 miles, and we estimate 3 h 05 min to 4 h 50 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Leicester and Southampton may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Southampton.

Tips for Leicester to Southampton coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
