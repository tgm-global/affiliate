Southampton to Bristol by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 1 h 45 min to 2 h 50 min for the 103 km between Southampton and Bristol. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Southampton and Bristol may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bristol.

Tips for Southampton to Bristol coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check where coaches stop in Southampton and Bristol. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
