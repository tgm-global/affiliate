Seattle to Birmingham at a glance

The straight-line distance between Seattle and Birmingham is about 4,685 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 8 h 25 min to 10 h 30 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Seattle

Seattle is served by Seattle-Tacoma (SEA).

Arriving in Birmingham

Birmingham is served by Birmingham (BHX).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Seattle to Birmingham flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Birmingham, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Seattle and Birmingham, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
