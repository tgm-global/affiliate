Brighton to Birmingham by coach at a glance

By road, Brighton to Birmingham is about 137 miles, and we estimate 3 h 25 min to 5 h 30 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Brighton and Birmingham may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Birmingham.

Tips for Brighton to Birmingham coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Brighton and Birmingham. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
