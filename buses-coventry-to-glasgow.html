Coventry to Glasgow by coach at a glance

By road, Coventry to Glasgow is about 263 miles, and we estimate 6 h 25 min to 10 h on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Coventry and Glasgow may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Glasgow.

Tips for Coventry to Glasgow coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Coventry and Glasgow. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
