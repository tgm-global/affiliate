Atlanta to Charlotte at a glance

The straight-line distance between Atlanta and Charlotte is about 227 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 55 min to 1 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Atlanta

Atlanta is served by Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL).

Arriving in Charlotte

Charlotte is served by Charlotte Douglas (CLT).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Atlanta to Charlotte flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Atlanta to Charlotte. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
