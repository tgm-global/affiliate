Dallas to Austin by bus at a glance

A bus from Dallas to Austin typically takes 4 h 20 min to 6 h 40 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Dallas and Austin may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Austin.

Tips for Dallas to Austin bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
