San Diego to Los Angeles by bus at a glance

A bus from San Diego to Los Angeles typically takes 2 h 45 min to 4 h 20 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

San Diego and Los Angeles may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Los Angeles.

Tips for San Diego to Los Angeles bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Diego and Los Angeles. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
