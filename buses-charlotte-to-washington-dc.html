Charlotte to Washington, DC by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 7 h 40 min to 11 h 35 min for the 530 km between Charlotte and Washington, DC. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Charlotte and Washington, DC may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Washington, DC.

Tips for Charlotte to Washington, DC bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Charlotte and Washington, DC. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
