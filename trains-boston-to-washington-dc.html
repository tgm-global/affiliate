Boston to Washington, DC by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from Boston to Washington, DC is about 394 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 5 h 10 min to 8 h 50 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

Boston: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Washington, DC: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Washington, DC.

Tips for Boston to Washington, DC train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
