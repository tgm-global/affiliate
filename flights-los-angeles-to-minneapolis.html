Los Angeles to Minneapolis at a glance

The straight-line distance between Los Angeles and Minneapolis is about 1,521 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 20 min to 4 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Los Angeles

Los Angeles is served by Los Angeles (LAX), Burbank (BUR) and Long Beach (LGB).

Arriving in Minneapolis

Minneapolis is served by Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Los Angeles to Minneapolis flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Los Angeles or Minneapolis.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
