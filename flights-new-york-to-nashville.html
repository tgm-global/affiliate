New York to Nashville at a glance

The straight-line distance between New York and Nashville is about 758 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 55 min to 2 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

Arriving in Nashville

Nashville is served by Nashville (BNA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to Nashville flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in New York or Nashville.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Nashville, especially if you arrive late at night.
