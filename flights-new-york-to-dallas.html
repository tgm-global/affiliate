New York to Dallas at a glance

Expect a flight of 3 h to 3 h 45 min for the 2,205 km between New York and Dallas. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

Arriving in Dallas

Dallas is served by Dallas Fort Worth (DFW) and Love Field (DAL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to Dallas flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Dallas, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around New York and Dallas, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
