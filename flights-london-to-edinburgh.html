London to Edinburgh at a glance

On the map, London and Edinburgh are roughly 533 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 05 min to 1 h 25 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

Arriving in Edinburgh

Edinburgh is served by Edinburgh (EDI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking London to Edinburgh flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around London and Edinburgh, so check each before you decide.
