Sheffield to Liverpool by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from Sheffield to Liverpool is about 63 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 50 min to 1 h 35 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Sheffield: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Liverpool: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Liverpool.

Tips for Sheffield to Liverpool train tickets

  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
