Philadelphia to Houston at a glance

The straight-line distance between Philadelphia and Houston is about 1,340 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h to 3 h 45 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Philadelphia to Houston flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Philadelphia and Houston, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Houston, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
