Derby to Coventry by train at a glance

A train from Derby to Coventry typically takes 30 min to 1 h. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Derby: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Coventry: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Coventry.

Tips for Derby to Coventry train tickets

  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Coventry. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
