Detroit to Houston at a glance

The straight-line distance between Detroit and Houston is about 1,106 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 35 min to 3 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Detroit

Detroit is served by Detroit Metropolitan (DTW).

Arriving in Houston

Houston is served by George Bush Intercontinental (IAH) and Hobby (HOU).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Detroit to Houston flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Detroit and Houston, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
