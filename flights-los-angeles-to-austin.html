Los Angeles to Austin at a glance

The straight-line distance between Los Angeles and Austin is about 1,225 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 45 min to 3 h 25 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Los Angeles and Austin, so check each before you decide.
