Tips for booking Atlanta to San Diego flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Atlanta or San Diego.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Atlanta and San Diego, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in San Diego, especially if you arrive late at night.
