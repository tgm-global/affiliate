Stops and arrival

San Diego and Phoenix may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Phoenix.

Tips for San Diego to Phoenix bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Diego and Phoenix. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
