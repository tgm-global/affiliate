San Francisco to Tampa at a glance

The straight-line distance between San Francisco and Tampa is about 2,396 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 4 h 55 min to 6 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

Arriving in Tampa

Tampa is served by Tampa (TPA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking San Francisco to Tampa flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Tampa, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in San Francisco or Tampa.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
