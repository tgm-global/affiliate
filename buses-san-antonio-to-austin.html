San Antonio to Austin by bus at a glance

By road, San Antonio to Austin is about 74 miles, and we estimate 1 h 55 min to 3 h 05 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

San Antonio and Austin may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Austin.

Tips for San Antonio to Austin bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
