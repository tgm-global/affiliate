New York to Atlanta at a glance

On the map, New York and Atlanta are roughly 1,199 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 55 min to 2 h 20 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Tips for booking New York to Atlanta flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Atlanta, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around New York and Atlanta, so check each before you decide.
