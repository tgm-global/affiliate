Wolverhampton to Stoke-on-Trent by coach at a glance

By road, Wolverhampton to Stoke-on-Trent is about 28 miles, and we estimate 50 min to 1 h 30 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Wolverhampton and Stoke-on-Trent may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Stoke-on-Trent.

Tips for Wolverhampton to Stoke-on-Trent coach tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
