Liverpool to Newcastle at a glance

On the map, Liverpool and Newcastle are roughly 195 km apart. A typical flying time is 45 min to 55 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Liverpool

Liverpool is served by Liverpool John Lennon (LPL).

Arriving in Newcastle

Newcastle is served by Newcastle (NCL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Liverpool to Newcastle flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Newcastle, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Liverpool or Newcastle.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Liverpool to Newcastle or a coach from Liverpool to Newcastle. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
