Stops and arrival

Las Vegas and San Francisco may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in San Francisco.

Tips for Las Vegas to San Francisco bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Las Vegas and San Francisco. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
