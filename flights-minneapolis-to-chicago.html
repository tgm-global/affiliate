Minneapolis to Chicago at a glance

On the map, Minneapolis and Chicago are roughly 570 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 10 min to 1 h 25 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Minneapolis

Minneapolis is served by Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP).

Arriving in Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Minneapolis to Chicago flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Minneapolis and Chicago, so check each before you decide.
