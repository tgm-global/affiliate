Plymouth to Bristol by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 2 h 35 min to 4 h 10 min for the 161 km between Plymouth and Bristol. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Plymouth and Bristol may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bristol.

Tips for Plymouth to Bristol coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
