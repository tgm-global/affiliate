New York to Washington, DC by train at a glance

For the roughly 327 km between New York and Washington, DC, expect a journey of 2 h 50 min to 4 h 50 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

New York: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Washington, DC: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Washington, DC.

Tips for New York to Washington, DC train tickets

  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Washington, DC and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
