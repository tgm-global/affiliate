Columbus to Indianapolis by bus at a glance

A bus from Columbus to Indianapolis typically takes 4 h 05 min to 6 h 15 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Columbus and Indianapolis may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Indianapolis.

Tips for Columbus to Indianapolis bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Columbus and Indianapolis. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
