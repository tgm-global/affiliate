San Francisco to Los Angeles at a glance

The straight-line distance between San Francisco and Los Angeles is about 347 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 10 min to 1 h 25 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

Arriving in Los Angeles

Los Angeles is served by Los Angeles (LAX), Burbank (BUR) and Long Beach (LGB).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking San Francisco to Los Angeles flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Los Angeles, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around San Francisco and Los Angeles, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in San Francisco or Los Angeles.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from San Francisco to Los Angeles or a coach from San Francisco to Los Angeles. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
