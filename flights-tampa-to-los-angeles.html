Tampa to Los Angeles at a glance

The straight-line distance between Tampa and Los Angeles is about 2,149 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 4 h 30 min to 5 h 35 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Tampa

Tampa is served by Tampa (TPA).

Arriving in Los Angeles

Los Angeles is served by Los Angeles (LAX), Burbank (BUR) and Long Beach (LGB).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Tampa to Los Angeles flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Los Angeles, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Tampa or Los Angeles.
