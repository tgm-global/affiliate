Tips for booking Detroit to Las Vegas flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Detroit and Las Vegas, so check each before you decide.

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
