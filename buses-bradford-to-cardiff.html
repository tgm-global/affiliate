Bradford to Cardiff by coach at a glance

A coach from Bradford to Cardiff typically takes 4 h 15 min to 6 h 40 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Bradford and Cardiff may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Cardiff.

Tips for Bradford to Cardiff coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Bradford and Cardiff. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
