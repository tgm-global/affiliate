Providence to Washington, DC by train at a glance

For the roughly 576 km between Providence and Washington, DC, expect a journey of 4 h 45 min to 8 h 05 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.
