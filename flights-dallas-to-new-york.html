Dallas to New York at a glance

The straight-line distance between Dallas and New York is about 1,370 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h to 3 h 45 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Dallas

Dallas is served by Dallas Fort Worth (DFW) and Love Field (DAL).

Arriving in New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Dallas to New York flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in New York, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
