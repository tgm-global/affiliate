Liverpool to Leicester by coach at a glance

By road, Liverpool to Leicester is about 94 miles, and we estimate 2 h 25 min to 3 h 55 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Liverpool and Leicester may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Leicester.

Tips for Liverpool to Leicester coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Liverpool and Leicester. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
