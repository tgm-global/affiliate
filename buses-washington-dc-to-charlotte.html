Stops and arrival

Washington, DC and Charlotte may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Charlotte.

Tips for Washington, DC to Charlotte bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Washington, DC and Charlotte. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
