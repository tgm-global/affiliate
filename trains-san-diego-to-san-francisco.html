San Diego to San Francisco by train at a glance

A train from San Diego to San Francisco typically takes 8 h 40 min to 14 h 45 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

San Diego: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. San Francisco: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in San Francisco.

Tips for San Diego to San Francisco train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in San Francisco and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
