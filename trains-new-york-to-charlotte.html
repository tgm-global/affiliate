New York to Charlotte by train at a glance

For the roughly 853 km between New York and Charlotte, expect a journey of 10 h to 17 h. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

New York: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Charlotte: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Charlotte.

Tips for New York to Charlotte train tickets

  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
