Glasgow to London by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 8 h 20 min to 13 h for the 554 km between Glasgow and London. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Glasgow and London may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in London.

Tips for Glasgow to London coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check where coaches stop in Glasgow and London. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
