Edinburgh to Coventry by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 6 h 10 min to 9 h 40 min for the 408 km between Edinburgh and Coventry. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Edinburgh and Coventry may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Coventry.

Tips for Edinburgh to Coventry coach tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Edinburgh and Coventry. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
