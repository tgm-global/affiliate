Plymouth to Hull by train at a glance

For the roughly 455 km between Plymouth and Hull, expect a journey of 3 h 35 min to 6 h 30 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Plymouth: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Hull: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Hull.

Tips for Plymouth to Hull train tickets

  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
