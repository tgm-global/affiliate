Atlanta to Washington, DC at a glance

The straight-line distance between Atlanta and Washington, DC is about 542 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 30 min to 1 h 50 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Atlanta

Atlanta is served by Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL).

Arriving in Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Atlanta to Washington, DC flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Atlanta and Washington, DC, so check each before you decide.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Atlanta or Washington, DC.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Atlanta to Washington, DC. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
