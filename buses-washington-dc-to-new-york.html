Washington, DC to New York by bus at a glance

A bus from Washington, DC to New York typically takes 4 h 50 min to 7 h 25 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Washington, DC and New York may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in New York.

Tips for Washington, DC to New York bus tickets

  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Washington, DC and New York. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
