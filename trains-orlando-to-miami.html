Orlando to Miami by train at a glance

For the roughly 330 km between Orlando and Miami, expect a journey of 4 h 05 min to 6 h 55 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Orlando: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Miami: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Miami.

Tips for Orlando to Miami train tickets

  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Miami and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
