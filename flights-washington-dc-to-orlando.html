Tips for booking Washington, DC to Orlando flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Washington, DC and Orlando, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Orlando, especially if you arrive late at night.
