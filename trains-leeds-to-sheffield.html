Leeds to Sheffield by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from Leeds to Sheffield is about 29 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 30 min to 50 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Leeds: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Sheffield: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Sheffield.

Tips for Leeds to Sheffield train tickets

  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
