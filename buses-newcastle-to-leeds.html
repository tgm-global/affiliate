Newcastle to Leeds by coach at a glance

A coach from Newcastle to Leeds typically takes 2 h 10 min to 3 h 25 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Newcastle and Leeds may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Leeds.

Tips for Newcastle to Leeds coach tickets

  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check where coaches stop in Newcastle and Leeds. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
