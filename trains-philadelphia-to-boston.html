Philadelphia to Boston by train at a glance

A train from Philadelphia to Boston typically takes 3 h 40 min to 6 h 15 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

Tips for Philadelphia to Boston train tickets

  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Boston and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
