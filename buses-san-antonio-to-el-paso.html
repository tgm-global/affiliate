San Antonio to El Paso by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 11 h 30 min to 17 h 25 min for the 808 km between San Antonio and El Paso. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

San Antonio and El Paso may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in El Paso.

Tips for San Antonio to El Paso bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Antonio and El Paso. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
