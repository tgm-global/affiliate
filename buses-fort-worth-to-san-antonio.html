Fort Worth to San Antonio by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 5 h 40 min to 8 h 40 min for the 387 km between Fort Worth and San Antonio. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Fort Worth and San Antonio may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in San Antonio.

Tips for Fort Worth to San Antonio bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Fort Worth and San Antonio. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
