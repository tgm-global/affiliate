Washington, DC to Chicago at a glance

The straight-line distance between Washington, DC and Chicago is about 593 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 35 min to 2 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

Arriving in Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Washington, DC to Chicago flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Washington, DC and Chicago, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Chicago, especially if you arrive late at night.

Other ways to travel
