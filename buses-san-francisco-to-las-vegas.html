San Francisco to Las Vegas by bus at a glance

By road, San Francisco to Las Vegas is about 417 miles, and we estimate 9 h 35 min to 14 h 30 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

San Francisco and Las Vegas may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Las Vegas.

Tips for San Francisco to Las Vegas bus tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Francisco and Las Vegas. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
