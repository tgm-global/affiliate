San Francisco to Minneapolis at a glance

The straight-line distance between San Francisco and Minneapolis is about 1,581 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 25 min to 4 h 15 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.
