Tampa to Miami at a glance

The straight-line distance between Tampa and Miami is about 206 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 55 min to 1 h 05 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Tampa to Miami flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Tampa and Miami, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Tampa or Miami.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
