Phoenix to San Diego at a glance

The straight-line distance between Phoenix and San Diego is about 299 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 05 min to 1 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Arriving in San Diego

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Phoenix to San Diego flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Phoenix and San Diego, so check each before you decide.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Phoenix or San Diego.
