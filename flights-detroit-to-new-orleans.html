Detroit to New Orleans at a glance

The straight-line distance between Detroit and New Orleans is about 940 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 15 min to 2 h 45 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Detroit

Detroit is served by Detroit Metropolitan (DTW).

Arriving in New Orleans

New Orleans is served by Louis Armstrong New Orleans (MSY).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Detroit to New Orleans flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Detroit or New Orleans.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
