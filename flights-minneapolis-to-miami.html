Minneapolis to Miami at a glance

The straight-line distance between Minneapolis and Miami is about 1,514 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 20 min to 4 h 05 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Minneapolis

Minneapolis is served by Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP).

Arriving in Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Minneapolis to Miami flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Miami, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Minneapolis or Miami.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Minneapolis and Miami, so check each before you decide.
