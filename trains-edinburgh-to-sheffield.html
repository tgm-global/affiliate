Edinburgh to Sheffield by train at a glance

For the roughly 306 km between Edinburgh and Sheffield, expect a journey of 2 h 25 min to 4 h 25 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Edinburgh: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Sheffield: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Sheffield.

Tips for Edinburgh to Sheffield train tickets

  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Sheffield. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
