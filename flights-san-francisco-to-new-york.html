San Francisco to New York at a glance

The straight-line distance between San Francisco and New York is about 2,566 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 5 h 15 min to 6 h 35 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

Arriving in New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking San Francisco to New York flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
