Charlotte to New York by train at a glance

A train from Charlotte to New York typically takes 10 h to 17 h. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Charlotte: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. New York: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in New York.

Tips for Charlotte to New York train tickets

  • Look at which station you will arrive at in New York and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
