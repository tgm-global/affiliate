Bristol to Wolverhampton by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 2 h 05 min to 3 h 25 min for the 130 km between Bristol and Wolverhampton. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Bristol and Wolverhampton may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Wolverhampton.

Tips for Bristol to Wolverhampton coach tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check where coaches stop in Bristol and Wolverhampton. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
