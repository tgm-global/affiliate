New York to Chicago at a glance

On the map, New York and Chicago are roughly 1,144 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 50 min to 2 h 15 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

Arriving in Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to Chicago flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Chicago, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in New York or Chicago.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around New York and Chicago, so check each before you decide.
