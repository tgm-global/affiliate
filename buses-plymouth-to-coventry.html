Plymouth to Coventry by coach at a glance

A coach from Plymouth to Coventry typically takes 4 h 30 min to 7 h 05 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Plymouth and Coventry may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Coventry.

Tips for Plymouth to Coventry coach tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Check where coaches stop in Plymouth and Coventry. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
