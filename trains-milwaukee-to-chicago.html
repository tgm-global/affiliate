Milwaukee to Chicago by train at a glance

For the roughly 131 km between Milwaukee and Chicago, expect a journey of 1 h 45 min to 3 h. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Milwaukee: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Chicago: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Chicago.

Tips for Milwaukee to Chicago train tickets

  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Chicago and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
