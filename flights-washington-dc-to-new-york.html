Washington, DC to New York at a glance

The straight-line distance between Washington, DC and New York is about 203 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 50 min to 1 h 05 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

Arriving in New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Washington, DC to New York flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Washington, DC or New York.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in New York, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
