Baltimore to Boston by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from Baltimore to Boston is about 359 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 4 h 45 min to 8 h 05 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

Baltimore: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Boston: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Boston.

Tips for Baltimore to Boston train tickets

  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
