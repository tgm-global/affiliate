Last updated: 1 November 2026

How we earn

We are a participant in affiliate programmes. When you click certain links and then buy a flight, train or bus ticket, tour, holiday package or taxi transfer from our partner, we may receive a commission.

No extra cost to you

Using our links does not increase the price you pay. Prices are set by our partners.

Link markings

Booking buttons use rel="sponsored nofollow" so that search engines can identify them as paid or affiliate links.

Editorial approach

Our guides are written to help you plan. Commission does not change the distances, time estimates or tips we publish. We do not claim that any option is the cheapest or the best, and we do not publish prices.

Legal context

This disclosure is intended to meet transparency rules for endorsements and affiliate marketing, including guidance from the US Federal Trade Commission and the UK Advertising Standards Authority and Competition and Markets Authority.

Your purchase

Your contract is with the partner, not with us. Questions about a booking, refunds or changes must be sent to that partner.
