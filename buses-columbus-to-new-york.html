Tips for Columbus to New York bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Columbus and New York. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
