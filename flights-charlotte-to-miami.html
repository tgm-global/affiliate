Charlotte to Miami at a glance

The straight-line distance between Charlotte and Miami is about 655 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 45 min to 2 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Charlotte

Charlotte is served by Charlotte Douglas (CLT).

Arriving in Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Charlotte to Miami flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Charlotte and Miami, so check each before you decide.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Miami, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
