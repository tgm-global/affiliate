Leeds to Glasgow by train at a glance

For the roughly 286 km between Leeds and Glasgow, expect a journey of 2 h 20 min to 4 h 10 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Leeds: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Glasgow: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Glasgow.

Tips for Leeds to Glasgow train tickets

  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Glasgow. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
