London to Glasgow at a glance

On the map, London and Glasgow are roughly 554 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 10 min to 1 h 25 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

Arriving in Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking London to Glasgow flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in London or Glasgow.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Glasgow, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from London to Glasgow or a coach from London to Glasgow. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
