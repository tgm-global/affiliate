Glasgow to Bradford by coach at a glance

By road, Glasgow to Bradford is about 173 miles, and we estimate 4 h 20 min to 6 h 45 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Glasgow and Bradford may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bradford.

Tips for Glasgow to Bradford coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Check where coaches stop in Glasgow and Bradford. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
