San Francisco to Atlanta at a glance

Expect a flight of 4 h 30 min to 5 h 35 min for the 3,436 km between San Francisco and Atlanta. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

Arriving in Atlanta

Atlanta is served by Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking San Francisco to Atlanta flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Atlanta, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in San Francisco or Atlanta.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
