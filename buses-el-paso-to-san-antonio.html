El Paso to San Antonio by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 11 h 30 min to 17 h 25 min for the 808 km between El Paso and San Antonio. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

El Paso and San Antonio may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in San Antonio.

Tips for El Paso to San Antonio bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in El Paso and San Antonio. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
