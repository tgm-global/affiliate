Miami to New Orleans at a glance

The straight-line distance between Miami and New Orleans is about 669 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 45 min to 2 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

Arriving in New Orleans

New Orleans is served by Louis Armstrong New Orleans (MSY).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Miami to New Orleans flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Miami or New Orleans.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Miami and New Orleans, so check each before you decide.
