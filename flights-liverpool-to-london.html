Liverpool to London at a glance

The straight-line distance between Liverpool and London is about 178 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 50 min to 1 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Liverpool

Liverpool is served by Liverpool John Lennon (LPL).

Arriving in London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Liverpool to London flights

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Liverpool and London, so check each before you decide.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Liverpool to London or a coach from Liverpool to London. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
