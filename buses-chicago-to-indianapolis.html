Chicago to Indianapolis by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 4 h to 6 h 05 min for the 265 km between Chicago and Indianapolis. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Chicago and Indianapolis may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Indianapolis.

Tips for Chicago to Indianapolis bus tickets

  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Chicago and Indianapolis. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
