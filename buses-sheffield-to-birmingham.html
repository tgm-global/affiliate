Sheffield to Birmingham by coach at a glance

A coach from Sheffield to Birmingham typically takes 1 h 45 min to 2 h 50 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Sheffield and Birmingham may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Birmingham.

Tips for Sheffield to Birmingham coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
