Chicago to Denver at a glance

The straight-line distance between Chicago and Denver is about 918 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 10 min to 2 h 45 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

Arriving in Denver

Denver is served by Denver (DEN).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Chicago to Denver flights

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Chicago or Denver.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
