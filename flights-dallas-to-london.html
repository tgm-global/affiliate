Dallas to London at a glance

Expect a flight of 8 h 30 min to 10 h 35 min for the 7,640 km between Dallas and London. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Dallas

Dallas is served by Dallas Fort Worth (DFW) and Love Field (DAL).

Arriving in London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Dallas to London flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Dallas or London.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in London, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Dallas and London, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
