Stops and arrival

Indianapolis and Nashville may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Nashville.

Tips for Indianapolis to Nashville bus tickets

  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Indianapolis and Nashville. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
