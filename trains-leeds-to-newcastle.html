Leeds to Newcastle by train at a glance

For the roughly 131 km between Leeds and Newcastle, expect a journey of 1 h 05 min to 2 h. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Leeds: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Newcastle: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Newcastle.

Tips for Leeds to Newcastle train tickets

  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Newcastle. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
