Belfast to Cardiff at a glance

Expect a flight of 55 min to 1 h 10 min for the 392 km between Belfast and Cardiff. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Belfast

Belfast is served by Belfast International (BFS) and George Best Belfast City (BHD).

Arriving in Cardiff

Cardiff is served by Cardiff (CWL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Belfast to Cardiff flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Cardiff, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Belfast or Cardiff.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
