Brighton to Newcastle by coach at a glance

A coach from Brighton to Newcastle typically takes 7 h 10 min to 11 h 10 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Brighton and Newcastle may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Newcastle.

Tips for Brighton to Newcastle coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Brighton and Newcastle. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
