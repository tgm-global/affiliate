Bradford to Leeds by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from Bradford to Leeds is about 9 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 10 min to 25 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Bradford: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Leeds: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Leeds.

Tips for Bradford to Leeds train tickets

  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Leeds. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
