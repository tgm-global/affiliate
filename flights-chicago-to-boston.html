Chicago to Boston at a glance

Expect a flight of 2 h 05 min to 2 h 35 min for the 1,365 km between Chicago and Boston. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

Arriving in Boston

Boston is served by Logan (BOS).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Chicago to Boston flights

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Chicago and Boston, so check each before you decide.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Boston, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
