Dallas to Seattle at a glance

On the map, Dallas and Seattle are roughly 2,702 km apart. A typical flying time is 3 h 35 min to 4 h 30 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Dallas

Dallas is served by Dallas Fort Worth (DFW) and Love Field (DAL).

Arriving in Seattle

Seattle is served by Seattle-Tacoma (SEA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Dallas to Seattle flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Dallas and Seattle, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Dallas or Seattle.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
