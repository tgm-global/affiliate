Minneapolis to Detroit by train at a glance

For the roughly 872 km between Minneapolis and Detroit, expect a journey of 10 h 15 min to 17 h 20 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Minneapolis: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Detroit: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Detroit.

Tips for Minneapolis to Detroit train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
