San Diego to San Francisco by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 10 h 30 min to 15 h 55 min for the 736 km between San Diego and San Francisco. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

San Diego and San Francisco may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in San Francisco.

Tips for San Diego to San Francisco bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Diego and San Francisco. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
