Nashville to Chicago by bus at a glance

By road, Nashville to Chicago is about 398 miles, and we estimate 9 h 10 min to 13 h 55 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Nashville and Chicago may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Chicago.

Tips for Nashville to Chicago bus tickets

  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
