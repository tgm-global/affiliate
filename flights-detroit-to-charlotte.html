Detroit to Charlotte at a glance

On the map, Detroit and Charlotte are roughly 812 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 25 min to 1 h 45 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Detroit

Detroit is served by Detroit Metropolitan (DTW).

Arriving in Charlotte

Charlotte is served by Charlotte Douglas (CLT).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Detroit to Charlotte flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Detroit or Charlotte.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Detroit and Charlotte, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Charlotte, especially if you arrive late at night.
