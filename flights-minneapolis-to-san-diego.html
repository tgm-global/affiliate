Minneapolis to San Diego at a glance

On the map, Minneapolis and San Diego are roughly 2,462 km apart. A typical flying time is 3 h 20 min to 4 h 10 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Tips for booking Minneapolis to San Diego flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Minneapolis and San Diego, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in San Diego, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
