Tips for booking Birmingham to Aberdeen flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Birmingham and Aberdeen, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Birmingham or Aberdeen.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
