San Francisco to Washington, DC at a glance

Expect a flight of 5 h to 6 h 15 min for the 3,918 km between San Francisco and Washington, DC. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

Arriving in Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking San Francisco to Washington, DC flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in San Francisco or Washington, DC.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Washington, DC, especially if you arrive late at night.
