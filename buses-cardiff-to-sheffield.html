Cardiff to Sheffield by coach at a glance

By road, Cardiff to Sheffield is about 150 miles, and we estimate 3 h 45 min to 5 h 55 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Cardiff and Sheffield may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Sheffield.

Tips for Cardiff to Sheffield coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Cardiff and Sheffield. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
