Boston to Cleveland by train at a glance

For the roughly 883 km between Boston and Cleveland, expect a journey of 10 h 20 min to 17 h 35 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.
