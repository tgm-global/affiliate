San Jose to San Diego by bus at a glance

By road, San Jose to San Diego is about 416 miles, and we estimate 9 h 35 min to 14 h 30 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

San Jose and San Diego may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in San Diego.

Tips for San Jose to San Diego bus tickets

  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in San Jose and San Diego. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
