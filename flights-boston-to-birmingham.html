Boston to Birmingham at a glance

On the map, Boston and Birmingham are roughly 5,116 km apart. A typical flying time is 5 h 55 min to 7 h 20 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Boston

Boston is served by Logan (BOS).

Arriving in Birmingham

Birmingham is served by Birmingham (BHX).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Boston to Birmingham flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Boston or Birmingham.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Boston and Birmingham, so check each before you decide.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
