Glasgow to Aberdeen at a glance

The straight-line distance between Glasgow and Aberdeen is about 121 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 45 min to 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

Arriving in Aberdeen

Aberdeen is served by Aberdeen (ABZ).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Glasgow to Aberdeen flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Glasgow and Aberdeen, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Aberdeen, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
