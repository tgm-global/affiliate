New York to Seattle at a glance

The straight-line distance between New York and Seattle is about 2,402 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 4 h 55 min to 6 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.
