Edinburgh to Leicester by coach at a glance

A coach from Edinburgh to Leicester typically takes 5 h 55 min to 9 h 20 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Edinburgh and Leicester may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Leicester.

Tips for Edinburgh to Leicester coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Check where coaches stop in Edinburgh and Leicester. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
