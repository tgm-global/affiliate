London to Bradford by coach at a glance

By road, London to Bradford is about 172 miles, and we estimate 4 h 15 min to 6 h 45 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

London and Bradford may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bradford.

Tips for London to Bradford coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in London and Bradford. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
