Baltimore to Chicago by train at a glance

A train from Baltimore to Chicago typically takes 11 h 20 min to 19 h 15 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Tips for Baltimore to Chicago train tickets

  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
