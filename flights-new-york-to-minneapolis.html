New York to Minneapolis at a glance

The straight-line distance between New York and Minneapolis is about 1,017 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 25 min to 3 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

Arriving in Minneapolis

Minneapolis is served by Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to Minneapolis flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
