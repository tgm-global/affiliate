Miami to Orlando by train at a glance

For the roughly 330 km between Miami and Orlando, expect a journey of 4 h 05 min to 6 h 55 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Miami: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Orlando: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Orlando.

Tips for Miami to Orlando train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
