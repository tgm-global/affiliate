Newcastle to Glasgow at a glance

The straight-line distance between Newcastle and Glasgow is about 120 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 45 min to 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Newcastle

Newcastle is served by Newcastle (NCL).

Arriving in Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Newcastle to Glasgow flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Newcastle or Glasgow.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Newcastle and Glasgow, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.

Other ways to travel
