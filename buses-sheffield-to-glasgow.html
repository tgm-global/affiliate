Sheffield to Glasgow by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 5 h to 7 h 55 min for the 328 km between Sheffield and Glasgow. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Sheffield and Glasgow may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Glasgow.

Tips for Sheffield to Glasgow coach tickets

  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
