San Francisco to Dallas at a glance

The straight-line distance between San Francisco and Dallas is about 1,481 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 15 min to 4 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

Arriving in Dallas

Dallas is served by Dallas Fort Worth (DFW) and Love Field (DAL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking San Francisco to Dallas flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in San Francisco or Dallas.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Dallas, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around San Francisco and Dallas, so check each before you decide.
