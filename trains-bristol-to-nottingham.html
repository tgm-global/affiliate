Bristol to Nottingham by train at a glance

A train from Bristol to Nottingham typically takes 1 h 35 min to 2 h 55 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Bristol: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Nottingham: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Nottingham.

Tips for Bristol to Nottingham train tickets

  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Nottingham. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
