Tips for booking San Francisco to Chicago flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Chicago, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in San Francisco or Chicago.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around San Francisco and Chicago, so check each before you decide.
