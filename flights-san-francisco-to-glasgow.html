San Francisco to Glasgow at a glance

The straight-line distance between San Francisco and Glasgow is about 5,023 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 9 h to 11 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

Arriving in Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking San Francisco to Glasgow flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in San Francisco or Glasgow.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Glasgow, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
