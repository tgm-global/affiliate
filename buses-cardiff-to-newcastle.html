Cardiff to Newcastle by coach at a glance

A coach from Cardiff to Newcastle typically takes 6 h 05 min to 9 h 35 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Cardiff and Newcastle may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Newcastle.

Tips for Cardiff to Newcastle coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check where coaches stop in Cardiff and Newcastle. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
