Birmingham to Plymouth by coach at a glance

By road, Birmingham to Plymouth is about 175 miles, and we estimate 4 h 20 min to 6 h 50 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Birmingham and Plymouth may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Plymouth.

Tips for Birmingham to Plymouth coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check where coaches stop in Birmingham and Plymouth. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
