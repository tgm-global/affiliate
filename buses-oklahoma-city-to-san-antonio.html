Oklahoma City to San Antonio by bus at a glance

By road, Oklahoma City to San Antonio is about 422 miles, and we estimate 9 h 45 min to 14 h 40 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Oklahoma City and San Antonio may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in San Antonio.

Tips for Oklahoma City to San Antonio bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Oklahoma City and San Antonio. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
