Cardiff to Derby by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 3 h 05 min to 4 h 55 min for the 197 km between Cardiff and Derby. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Cardiff and Derby may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Derby.

Tips for Cardiff to Derby coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Check where coaches stop in Cardiff and Derby. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
