Detroit to Boston by train at a glance

A train from Detroit to Boston typically takes 11 h 30 min to 19 h 30 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Detroit: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Boston: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Boston.

Tips for Detroit to Boston train tickets

  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Boston and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
