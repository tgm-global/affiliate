New Orleans to Houston at a glance

The straight-line distance between New Orleans and Houston is about 318 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 05 min to 1 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from New Orleans

New Orleans is served by Louis Armstrong New Orleans (MSY).

Arriving in Houston

Houston is served by George Bush Intercontinental (IAH) and Hobby (HOU).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New Orleans to Houston flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in New Orleans or Houston.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Houston, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
