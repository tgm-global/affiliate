New York to Denver at a glance

Expect a flight of 3 h 30 min to 4 h 25 min for the 2,618 km between New York and Denver. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Arriving in Denver

Denver is served by Denver (DEN).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to Denver flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in New York or Denver.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
