Glasgow to Sheffield by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from Glasgow to Sheffield is about 204 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 2 h 35 min to 4 h 45 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Glasgow: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Sheffield: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Sheffield.

Tips for Glasgow to Sheffield train tickets

  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Sheffield. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
