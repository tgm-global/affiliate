Glasgow to Newcastle by coach at a glance

A coach from Glasgow to Newcastle typically takes 3 h to 4 h 50 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Glasgow and Newcastle may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Newcastle.

Tips for Glasgow to Newcastle coach tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check where coaches stop in Glasgow and Newcastle. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
