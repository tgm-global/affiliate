Glasgow to Newcastle at a glance

On the map, Glasgow and Newcastle are roughly 192 km apart. A typical flying time is 45 min to 55 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

Arriving in Newcastle

Newcastle is served by Newcastle (NCL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Glasgow to Newcastle flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Glasgow and Newcastle, so check each before you decide.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Newcastle, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.

Other ways to travel
