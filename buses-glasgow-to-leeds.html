Glasgow to Leeds by coach at a glance

A coach from Glasgow to Leeds typically takes 4 h 25 min to 6 h 55 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Glasgow and Leeds may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Leeds.

Tips for Glasgow to Leeds coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
