Brighton to Cardiff by coach at a glance

By road, Brighton to Cardiff is about 139 miles, and we estimate 3 h 30 min to 5 h 35 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Brighton and Cardiff may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Cardiff.

Tips for Brighton to Cardiff coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
