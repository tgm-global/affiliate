Pittsburgh to Philadelphia by train at a glance

For the roughly 413 km between Pittsburgh and Philadelphia, expect a journey of 5 h to 8 h 30 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Pittsburgh: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Philadelphia: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Philadelphia.

Tips for Pittsburgh to Philadelphia train tickets

  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Philadelphia and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
