Cardiff to Belfast at a glance

The straight-line distance between Cardiff and Belfast is about 244 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 55 min to 1 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Cardiff

Cardiff is served by Cardiff (CWL).

Arriving in Belfast

Belfast is served by Belfast International (BFS) and George Best Belfast City (BHD).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Cardiff to Belfast flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Belfast, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
