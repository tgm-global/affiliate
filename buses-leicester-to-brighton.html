Leicester to Brighton by coach at a glance

A coach from Leicester to Brighton typically takes 3 h 20 min to 5 h 20 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Leicester and Brighton may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Brighton.

Tips for Leicester to Brighton coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Check where coaches stop in Leicester and Brighton. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
