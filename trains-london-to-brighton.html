London to Brighton by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from London to Brighton is about 48 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 40 min to 1 h 15 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

London: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Brighton: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Brighton.

Tips for London to Brighton train tickets

  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Brighton. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
