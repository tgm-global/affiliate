Birmingham to Stoke-on-Trent by coach at a glance

By road, Birmingham to Stoke-on-Trent is about 38 miles, and we estimate 1 h 05 min to 1 h 50 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Birmingham and Stoke-on-Trent may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Stoke-on-Trent.

Tips for Birmingham to Stoke-on-Trent coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check where coaches stop in Birmingham and Stoke-on-Trent. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
