Philadelphia to Belfast at a glance

The straight-line distance between Philadelphia and Belfast is about 3,254 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 6 h to 7 h 30 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Philadelphia

Philadelphia is served by Philadelphia (PHL).

Arriving in Belfast

Belfast is served by Belfast International (BFS) and George Best Belfast City (BHD).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Philadelphia to Belfast flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Philadelphia or Belfast.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Belfast, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
