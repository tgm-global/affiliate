Nashville to Las Vegas at a glance

The straight-line distance between Nashville and Las Vegas is about 1,576 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 25 min to 4 h 15 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Nashville to Las Vegas flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Nashville or Las Vegas.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
