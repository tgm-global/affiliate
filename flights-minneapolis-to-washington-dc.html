Minneapolis to Washington, DC at a glance

The straight-line distance between Minneapolis and Washington, DC is about 931 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 15 min to 2 h 45 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Minneapolis

Minneapolis is served by Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP).

Arriving in Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Minneapolis to Washington, DC flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Washington, DC, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Minneapolis or Washington, DC.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
