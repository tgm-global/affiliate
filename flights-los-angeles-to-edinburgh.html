Los Angeles to Edinburgh at a glance

The straight-line distance between Los Angeles and Edinburgh is about 5,146 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 9 h 10 min to 11 h 25 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Los Angeles

Los Angeles is served by Los Angeles (LAX), Burbank (BUR) and Long Beach (LGB).

Arriving in Edinburgh

Edinburgh is served by Edinburgh (EDI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Los Angeles to Edinburgh flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Los Angeles or Edinburgh.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Los Angeles and Edinburgh, so check each before you decide.
