Charlotte to Detroit at a glance

The straight-line distance between Charlotte and Detroit is about 505 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 25 min to 1 h 45 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Charlotte

Charlotte is served by Charlotte Douglas (CLT).

Arriving in Detroit

Detroit is served by Detroit Metropolitan (DTW).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Charlotte to Detroit flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Charlotte or Detroit.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Charlotte and Detroit, so check each before you decide.

Other ways to travel
