Boston to New York at a glance

Tips for booking Boston to New York flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Boston and New York, so check each before you decide.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in New York, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
