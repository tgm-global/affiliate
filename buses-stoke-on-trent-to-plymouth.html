Stoke-on-Trent to Plymouth by coach at a glance

By road, Stoke-on-Trent to Plymouth is about 200 miles, and we estimate 4 h 55 min to 7 h 45 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Stoke-on-Trent and Plymouth may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Plymouth.

Tips for Stoke-on-Trent to Plymouth coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Stoke-on-Trent and Plymouth. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
