Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. This page explains how they relate to this site.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Cookies on this site

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies itself. Your browser may store technical files, such as cached styles and scripts, to load pages faster.

Affiliate cookies

When you click an affiliate link, the partner's website may set a cookie so that a purchase can be linked to us for commission. That cookie belongs to the partner and is covered by its policy.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how partner websites work.

Changes

If we add analytics or other tools in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent.
