London to Leicester by train at a glance

A train from London to Leicester typically takes 1 h 10 min to 2 h 10 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

London: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Leicester: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Leicester.

Tips for London to Leicester train tickets

  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Leicester. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
