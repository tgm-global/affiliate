Washington, DC to New York by train at a glance

For the roughly 327 km between Washington, DC and New York, expect a journey of 2 h 50 min to 4 h 50 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

Washington, DC: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. New York: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in New York.

Tips for Washington, DC to New York train tickets

  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
