Stops and arrival

Indianapolis and Washington, DC may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Washington, DC.

Tips for Indianapolis to Washington, DC bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Indianapolis and Washington, DC. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
