San Francisco to Boston at a glance

Expect a flight of 5 h 30 min to 6 h 50 min for the 4,333 km between San Francisco and Boston. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Tips for booking San Francisco to Boston flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around San Francisco and Boston, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in San Francisco or Boston.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
