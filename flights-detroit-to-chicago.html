Detroit to Chicago at a glance

The straight-line distance between Detroit and Chicago is about 237 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 55 min to 1 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Detroit

Detroit is served by Detroit Metropolitan (DTW).

Arriving in Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Detroit to Chicago flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Detroit and Chicago, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Chicago, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
