Newcastle to Liverpool at a glance

On the map, Newcastle and Liverpool are roughly 195 km apart. A typical flying time is 45 min to 55 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Newcastle is served by Newcastle (NCL).

Arriving in Liverpool

Liverpool is served by Liverpool John Lennon (LPL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Newcastle to Liverpool flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Newcastle and Liverpool, so check each before you decide.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Liverpool, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Newcastle or Liverpool.

Other ways to travel
