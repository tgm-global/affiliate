Coventry to Bristol by train at a glance

A train from Coventry to Bristol typically takes 1 h 05 min to 2 h. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Coventry: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Bristol: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Bristol.

Tips for Coventry to Bristol train tickets

  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Bristol. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
