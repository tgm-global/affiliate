Birmingham to Hull by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 2 h 45 min to 4 h 25 min for the 174 km between Birmingham and Hull. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Birmingham and Hull may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Hull.

Tips for Birmingham to Hull coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check where coaches stop in Birmingham and Hull. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
