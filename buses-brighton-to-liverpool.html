Brighton to Liverpool by coach at a glance

By road, Brighton to Liverpool is about 216 miles, and we estimate 5 h 20 min to 8 h 20 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Brighton and Liverpool may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Liverpool.

Tips for Brighton to Liverpool coach tickets

  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Check where coaches stop in Brighton and Liverpool. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
