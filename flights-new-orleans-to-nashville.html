New Orleans to Nashville at a glance

The straight-line distance between New Orleans and Nashville is about 469 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 20 min to 1 h 40 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking New Orleans to Nashville flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around New Orleans and Nashville, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
