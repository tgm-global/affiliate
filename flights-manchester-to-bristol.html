Tips for booking Manchester to Bristol flights

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Manchester and Bristol, so check each before you decide.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Bristol, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
