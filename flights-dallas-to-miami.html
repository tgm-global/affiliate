Tips for booking Dallas to Miami flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Dallas and Miami, so check each before you decide.

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Miami, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Dallas or Miami.
