Types of tours to look for in Dundee

Guided walking tours

A local guide can explain the history and layout of the centre in two to three hours, which is a useful first-day activity.

Evening and night tours

Evening walks and transport-based sightseeing can suit travellers who prefer to avoid daytime heat or crowds.

Hop-on hop-off sightseeing

Where offered, multi-stop routes help you cover the main sights at your own pace.

Day trips to nearby places

Coach or minibus excursions let you see places outside the centre without arranging your own transport.

Museum and heritage visits

Timed-entry tickets and guided visits can save queueing at popular attractions.
