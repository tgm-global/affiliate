Providence to New York by train at a glance

For the roughly 249 km between Providence and New York, expect a journey of 2 h 15 min to 3 h 45 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

Providence: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. New York: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in New York.

Tips for Providence to New York train tickets

  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in New York and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
