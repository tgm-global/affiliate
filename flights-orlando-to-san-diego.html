Orlando to San Diego at a glance

The straight-line distance between Orlando and San Diego is about 2,137 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 4 h 30 min to 5 h 35 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Orlando to San Diego flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Orlando and San Diego, so check each before you decide.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Orlando or San Diego.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in San Diego, especially if you arrive late at night.
