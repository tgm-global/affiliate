Manchester to Liverpool by coach at a glance

By road, Manchester to Liverpool is about 31 miles, and we estimate 55 min to 1 h 40 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Manchester and Liverpool may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Liverpool.

Tips for Manchester to Liverpool coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Manchester and Liverpool. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
