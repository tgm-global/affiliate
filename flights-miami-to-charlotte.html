Miami to Charlotte at a glance

The straight-line distance between Miami and Charlotte is about 655 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 45 min to 2 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

Arriving in Charlotte

Charlotte is served by Charlotte Douglas (CLT).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Miami to Charlotte flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Miami and Charlotte, so check each before you decide.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Charlotte, especially if you arrive late at night.
