Nottingham to Wolverhampton by train at a glance

For the roughly 77 km between Nottingham and Wolverhampton, expect a journey of 40 min to 1 h 15 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Nottingham: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Wolverhampton: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Wolverhampton.

Tips for Nottingham to Wolverhampton train tickets

  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
