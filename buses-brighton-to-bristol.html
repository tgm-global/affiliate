Brighton to Bristol by coach at a glance

A coach from Brighton to Bristol typically takes 2 h 55 min to 4 h 40 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Brighton and Bristol may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bristol.

Tips for Brighton to Bristol coach tickets

  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check where coaches stop in Brighton and Bristol. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
