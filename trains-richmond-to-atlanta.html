Richmond to Atlanta by train at a glance

For the roughly 755 km between Richmond and Atlanta, expect a journey of 8 h 55 min to 15 h 05 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Tips for Richmond to Atlanta train tickets

  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
