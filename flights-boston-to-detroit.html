Boston to Detroit at a glance

The straight-line distance between Boston and Detroit is about 612 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 40 min to 2 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Boston

Boston is served by Logan (BOS).

Arriving in Detroit

Detroit is served by Detroit Metropolitan (DTW).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Boston to Detroit flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Boston and Detroit, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Boston or Detroit.
