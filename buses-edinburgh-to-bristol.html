Edinburgh to Bristol by coach at a glance

A coach from Edinburgh to Bristol typically takes 7 h 35 min to 11 h 50 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Edinburgh and Bristol may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bristol.

Tips for Edinburgh to Bristol coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Edinburgh and Bristol. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
