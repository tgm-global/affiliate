Richmond to New York by train at a glance

For the roughly 460 km between Richmond and New York, expect a journey of 5 h 30 min to 9 h 20 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Richmond: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. New York: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in New York.

Tips for Richmond to New York train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in New York and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
