Plymouth to Hull by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 6 h 55 min to 10 h 45 min for the 455 km between Plymouth and Hull. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Plymouth and Hull may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Hull.

Tips for Plymouth to Hull coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check where coaches stop in Plymouth and Hull. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
