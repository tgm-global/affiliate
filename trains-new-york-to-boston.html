New York to Boston by train at a glance

A train from New York to Boston typically takes 2 h 40 min to 4 h 30 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

New York: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Boston: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Boston.

Tips for New York to Boston train tickets

  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Boston and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
