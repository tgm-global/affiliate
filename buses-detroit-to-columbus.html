Detroit to Columbus by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 4 h to 6 h 05 min for the 263 km between Detroit and Columbus. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Detroit and Columbus may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Columbus.

Tips for Detroit to Columbus bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Detroit and Columbus. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
