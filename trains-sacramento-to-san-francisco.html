Sacramento to San Francisco by train at a glance

For the roughly 121 km between Sacramento and San Francisco, expect a journey of 1 h 40 min to 2 h 50 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Sacramento: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. San Francisco: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in San Francisco.

Tips for Sacramento to San Francisco train tickets

  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
