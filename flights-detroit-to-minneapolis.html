Tips for booking Detroit to Minneapolis flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Detroit and Minneapolis, so check each before you decide.

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Minneapolis, especially if you arrive late at night.
