Minneapolis to Boston at a glance

Expect a flight of 2 h 35 min to 3 h 10 min for the 1,804 km between Minneapolis and Boston. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Minneapolis

Minneapolis is served by Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP).

Arriving in Boston

Boston is served by Logan (BOS).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Minneapolis to Boston flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Minneapolis or Boston.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Boston, especially if you arrive late at night.
