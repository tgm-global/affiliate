Minneapolis to Seattle at a glance

On the map, Minneapolis and Seattle are roughly 2,238 km apart. A typical flying time is 3 h 05 min to 3 h 50 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Minneapolis

Minneapolis is served by Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP).

Arriving in Seattle

Seattle is served by Seattle-Tacoma (SEA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Minneapolis to Seattle flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Seattle, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Minneapolis and Seattle, so check each before you decide.
