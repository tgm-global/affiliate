Birmingham to Coventry by coach at a glance

By road, Birmingham to Coventry is about 17 miles, and we estimate 35 min to 1 h 05 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Birmingham and Coventry may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Coventry.

Tips for Birmingham to Coventry coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check where coaches stop in Birmingham and Coventry. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
