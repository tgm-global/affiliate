Washington, DC to Indianapolis by bus at a glance

By road, Washington, DC to Indianapolis is about 491 miles, and we estimate 11 h 15 min to 17 h on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Washington, DC and Indianapolis may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Indianapolis.

Tips for Washington, DC to Indianapolis bus tickets

  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Washington, DC and Indianapolis. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
