Hartford to Washington, DC by train at a glance

For the roughly 487 km between Hartford and Washington, DC, expect a journey of 4 h to 6 h 55 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Tips for Hartford to Washington, DC train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Washington, DC and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
