Minneapolis to Atlanta at a glance

The straight-line distance between Minneapolis and Atlanta is about 908 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 10 min to 2 h 45 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Minneapolis

Minneapolis is served by Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP).

Arriving in Atlanta

Atlanta is served by Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Minneapolis to Atlanta flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Minneapolis and Atlanta, so check each before you decide.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Atlanta, especially if you arrive late at night.
