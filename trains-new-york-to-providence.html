New York to Providence by train at a glance

For the roughly 249 km between New York and Providence, expect a journey of 2 h 15 min to 3 h 45 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

New York: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Providence: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Providence.

Tips for New York to Providence train tickets

  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
