Detroit to Miami at a glance

On the map, Detroit and Miami are roughly 1,860 km apart. A typical flying time is 2 h 40 min to 3 h 15 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Detroit

Detroit is served by Detroit Metropolitan (DTW).

Arriving in Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Detroit to Miami flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Detroit or Miami.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Detroit and Miami, so check each before you decide.
