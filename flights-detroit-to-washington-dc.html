Detroit to Washington, DC at a glance

Expect a flight of 1 h 15 min to 1 h 30 min for the 633 km between Detroit and Washington, DC. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Detroit

Detroit is served by Detroit Metropolitan (DTW).

Arriving in Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Detroit to Washington, DC flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
