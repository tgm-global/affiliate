Houston to Chicago at a glance

On the map, Houston and Chicago are roughly 1,516 km apart. A typical flying time is 2 h 15 min to 2 h 50 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Houston to Chicago flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Houston or Chicago.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Chicago, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
