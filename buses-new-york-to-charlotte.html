New York to Charlotte by bus at a glance

By road, New York to Charlotte is about 530 miles, and we estimate 12 h 10 min to 18 h 20 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

New York and Charlotte may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Charlotte.

Tips for New York to Charlotte bus tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in New York and Charlotte. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
