Nashville to Charlotte by bus at a glance

A bus from Nashville to Charlotte typically takes 7 h 55 min to 11 h 55 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Nashville and Charlotte may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Charlotte.

Tips for Nashville to Charlotte bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Nashville and Charlotte. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
