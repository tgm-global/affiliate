Miami to Orlando at a glance

Expect a flight of 55 min to 1 h 05 min for the 330 km between Miami and Orlando. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Miami to Orlando flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Miami and Orlando, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Miami or Orlando.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
