Las Vegas to Los Angeles by bus at a glance

By road, Las Vegas to Los Angeles is about 228 miles, and we estimate 5 h 25 min to 8 h 15 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Las Vegas and Los Angeles may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Los Angeles.

Tips for Las Vegas to Los Angeles bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
