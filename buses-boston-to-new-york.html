Boston to New York by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 4 h 35 min to 7 h for the 306 km between Boston and New York. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Boston and New York may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in New York.

Tips for Boston to New York bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
