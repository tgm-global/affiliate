Chicago to Washington, DC by train at a glance

A train from Chicago to Washington, DC typically takes 11 h 10 min to 18 h 55 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Chicago: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Washington, DC: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Washington, DC.

Tips for Chicago to Washington, DC train tickets

  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Washington, DC and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
