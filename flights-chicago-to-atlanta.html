Chicago to Atlanta at a glance

On the map, Chicago and Atlanta are roughly 947 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 35 min to 2 h, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

Arriving in Atlanta

Atlanta is served by Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Chicago to Atlanta flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Atlanta, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Chicago and Atlanta, so check each before you decide.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Chicago to Atlanta. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
