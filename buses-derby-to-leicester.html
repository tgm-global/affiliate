Derby to Leicester by coach at a glance

By road, Derby to Leicester is about 24 miles, and we estimate 45 min to 1 h 25 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Derby and Leicester may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Leicester.

Tips for Derby to Leicester coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check where coaches stop in Derby and Leicester. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
