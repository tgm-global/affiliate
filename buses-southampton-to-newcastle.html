Southampton to Newcastle by coach at a glance

A coach from Southampton to Newcastle typically takes 6 h 55 min to 10 h 45 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Southampton and Newcastle may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Newcastle.

Tips for Southampton to Newcastle coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check where coaches stop in Southampton and Newcastle. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
