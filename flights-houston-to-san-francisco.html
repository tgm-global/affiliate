Houston to San Francisco at a glance

The straight-line distance between Houston and San Francisco is about 1,642 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 35 min to 4 h 25 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.
