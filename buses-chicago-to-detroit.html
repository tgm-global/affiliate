Chicago to Detroit by bus at a glance

By road, Chicago to Detroit is about 237 miles, and we estimate 5 h 35 min to 8 h 30 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Chicago and Detroit may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Detroit.

Tips for Chicago to Detroit bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
