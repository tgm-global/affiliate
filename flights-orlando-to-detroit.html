Orlando to Detroit at a glance

The straight-line distance between Orlando and Detroit is about 957 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 15 min to 2 h 50 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Orlando to Detroit flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Detroit, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
