Tampa to Minneapolis at a glance

The straight-line distance between Tampa and Minneapolis is about 1,318 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 55 min to 3 h 40 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Arriving in Minneapolis

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Tampa to Minneapolis flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Tampa or Minneapolis.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Tampa and Minneapolis, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
