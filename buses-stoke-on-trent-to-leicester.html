Stoke-on-Trent to Leicester by coach at a glance

By road, Stoke-on-Trent to Leicester is about 50 miles, and we estimate 1 h 25 min to 2 h 20 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Stoke-on-Trent and Leicester may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Leicester.

Tips for Stoke-on-Trent to Leicester coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Stoke-on-Trent and Leicester. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
