Bristol to Manchester at a glance

The straight-line distance between Bristol and Manchester is about 141 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 45 min to 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Bristol

Bristol is served by Bristol (BRS).

Arriving in Manchester

Manchester is served by Manchester (MAN).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Bristol to Manchester flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Manchester, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Bristol to Manchester or a coach from Bristol to Manchester. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
