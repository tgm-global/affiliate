Phoenix to Orlando at a glance

The straight-line distance between Phoenix and Orlando is about 1,842 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 55 min to 4 h 50 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Arriving in Orlando

Orlando is served by Orlando (MCO).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Phoenix to Orlando flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Phoenix and Orlando, so check each before you decide.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Orlando, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
