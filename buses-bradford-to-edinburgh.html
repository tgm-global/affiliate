Bradford to Edinburgh by coach at a glance

A coach from Bradford to Edinburgh typically takes 4 h to 6 h 15 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Bradford and Edinburgh may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Edinburgh.

Tips for Bradford to Edinburgh coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check where coaches stop in Bradford and Edinburgh. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
