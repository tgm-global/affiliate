Philadelphia to New York by bus at a glance

By road, Philadelphia to New York is about 81 miles, and we estimate 2 h 05 min to 3 h 20 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Philadelphia and New York may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in New York.

Tips for Philadelphia to New York bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
