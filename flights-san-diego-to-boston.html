Tips for booking San Diego to Boston flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around San Diego and Boston, so check each before you decide.

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in San Diego or Boston.
