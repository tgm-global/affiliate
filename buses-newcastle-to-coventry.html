Newcastle to Coventry by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 4 h 25 min to 6 h 55 min for the 285 km between Newcastle and Coventry. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Newcastle and Coventry may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Coventry.

Tips for Newcastle to Coventry coach tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Newcastle and Coventry. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
