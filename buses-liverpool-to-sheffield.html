Liverpool to Sheffield by coach at a glance

A coach from Liverpool to Sheffield typically takes 1 h 40 min to 2 h 45 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Liverpool and Sheffield may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Sheffield.

Tips for Liverpool to Sheffield coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check where coaches stop in Liverpool and Sheffield. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
