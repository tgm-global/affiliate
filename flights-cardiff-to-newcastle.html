Cardiff to Newcastle at a glance

The straight-line distance between Cardiff and Newcastle is about 250 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h to 1 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Cardiff to Newcastle flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Newcastle, especially if you arrive late at night.
