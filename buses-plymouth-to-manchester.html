Plymouth to Manchester by coach at a glance

A coach from Plymouth to Manchester typically takes 5 h 40 min to 8 h 50 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Plymouth and Manchester may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Manchester.

Tips for Plymouth to Manchester coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
