Edinburgh to Belfast at a glance

Expect a flight of 45 min to 55 min for the 229 km between Edinburgh and Belfast. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Edinburgh

Edinburgh is served by Edinburgh (EDI).

Arriving in Belfast

Belfast is served by Belfast International (BFS) and George Best Belfast City (BHD).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Edinburgh to Belfast flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Edinburgh or Belfast.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Edinburgh and Belfast, so check each before you decide.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
