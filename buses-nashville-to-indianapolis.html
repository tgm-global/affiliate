Stops and arrival

Nashville and Indianapolis may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Indianapolis.

Tips for Nashville to Indianapolis bus tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Nashville and Indianapolis. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
