London to Liverpool at a glance

Expect a flight of 50 min to 1 h for the 286 km between London and Liverpool. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

Arriving in Liverpool

Liverpool is served by Liverpool John Lennon (LPL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking London to Liverpool flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in London or Liverpool.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around London and Liverpool, so check each before you decide.
