Newcastle to Plymouth by coach at a glance

A coach from Newcastle to Plymouth typically takes 8 h 10 min to 12 h 40 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Newcastle and Plymouth may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Plymouth.

Tips for Newcastle to Plymouth coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Newcastle and Plymouth. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
