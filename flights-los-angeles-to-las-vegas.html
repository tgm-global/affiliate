Los Angeles to Las Vegas at a glance

The straight-line distance between Los Angeles and Las Vegas is about 228 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 55 min to 1 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.
