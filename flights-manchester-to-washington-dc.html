Manchester to Washington, DC at a glance

The straight-line distance between Manchester and Washington, DC is about 3,540 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 7 h 15 min to 9 h 05 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Manchester

Manchester is served by Manchester (MAN).

Arriving in Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Manchester to Washington, DC flights

  • Check US entry requirements well before you travel. Depending on your nationality you may need an ESTA or a visa.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Westbound daytime flights often suit a first day of sightseeing, while overnight flights suit people who can sleep on board.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Manchester and Washington, DC, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
