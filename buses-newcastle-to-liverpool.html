Newcastle to Liverpool by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 3 h 05 min to 4 h 55 min for the 195 km between Newcastle and Liverpool. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Newcastle and Liverpool may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Liverpool.

Tips for Newcastle to Liverpool coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
