Brighton to Bradford by coach at a glance

A coach from Brighton to Bradford typically takes 5 h 20 min to 8 h 20 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Brighton and Bradford may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bradford.

Tips for Brighton to Bradford coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check where coaches stop in Brighton and Bradford. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
