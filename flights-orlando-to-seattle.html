Tips for booking Orlando to Seattle flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Seattle, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Orlando and Seattle, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
