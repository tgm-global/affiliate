Wolverhampton to Coventry by coach at a glance

By road, Wolverhampton to Coventry is about 29 miles, and we estimate 55 min to 1 h 35 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Wolverhampton and Coventry may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Coventry.

Tips for Wolverhampton to Coventry coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Wolverhampton and Coventry. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
