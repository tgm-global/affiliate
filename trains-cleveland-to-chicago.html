Cleveland to Chicago by train at a glance

For the roughly 494 km between Cleveland and Chicago, expect a journey of 5 h 55 min to 10 h 05 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Cleveland: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Chicago: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Chicago.

Tips for Cleveland to Chicago train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Chicago and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
