New York to Detroit by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 11 h 05 min to 16 h 40 min for the 773 km between New York and Detroit. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

New York and Detroit may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Detroit.

Tips for New York to Detroit bus tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
