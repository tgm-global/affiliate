Boston to Washington, DC by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 9 h 05 min to 13 h 45 min for the 633 km between Boston and Washington, DC. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Boston and Washington, DC may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Washington, DC.

Tips for Boston to Washington, DC bus tickets

  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Boston and Washington, DC. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
