New York to Columbus by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 10 h 55 min to 16 h 30 min for the 766 km between New York and Columbus. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

New York and Columbus may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Columbus.

Tips for New York to Columbus bus tickets

  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in New York and Columbus. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
