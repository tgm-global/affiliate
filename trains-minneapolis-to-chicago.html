Minneapolis to Chicago by train at a glance

The straight-line distance from Minneapolis to Chicago is about 355 miles, but the railway is usually longer than the direct line. We estimate 6 h 45 min to 11 h 30 min for the trip, which varies with the service, stops and any change. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Minneapolis: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Chicago: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Chicago.

Tips for Minneapolis to Chicago train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Chicago and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
