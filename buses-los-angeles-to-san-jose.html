Los Angeles to San Jose by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 7 h 10 min to 10 h 50 min for the 492 km between Los Angeles and San Jose. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Los Angeles and San Jose may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in San Jose.

Tips for Los Angeles to San Jose bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
