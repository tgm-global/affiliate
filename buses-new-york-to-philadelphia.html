New York to Philadelphia by bus at a glance

By road, New York to Philadelphia is about 81 miles, and we estimate 2 h 05 min to 3 h 20 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

New York and Philadelphia may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Philadelphia.

Tips for New York to Philadelphia bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in New York and Philadelphia. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
