Nashville to Detroit by bus at a glance

By road, Nashville to Detroit is about 471 miles, and we estimate 10 h 50 min to 16 h 20 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Nashville and Detroit may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Detroit.

Tips for Nashville to Detroit bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
