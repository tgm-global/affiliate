New York to Orlando at a glance

Expect a flight of 2 h 15 min to 2 h 45 min for the 1,510 km between New York and Orlando. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

Arriving in Orlando

Orlando is served by Orlando (MCO).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to Orlando flights

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around New York and Orlando, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in New York or Orlando.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
