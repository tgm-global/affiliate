Hull to Sheffield by train at a glance

For the roughly 84 km between Hull and Sheffield, expect a journey of 45 min to 1 h 25 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Hull: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Sheffield: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Sheffield.

Tips for Hull to Sheffield train tickets

  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Sheffield. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
