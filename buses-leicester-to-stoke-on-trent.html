Leicester to Stoke-on-Trent by coach at a glance

A coach from Leicester to Stoke-on-Trent typically takes 1 h 25 min to 2 h 20 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Leicester and Stoke-on-Trent may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Stoke-on-Trent.

Tips for Leicester to Stoke-on-Trent coach tickets

  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
