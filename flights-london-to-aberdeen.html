London to Aberdeen at a glance

The straight-line distance between London and Aberdeen is about 398 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 15 min to 1 h 30 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

Arriving in Aberdeen

Aberdeen is served by Aberdeen (ABZ).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking London to Aberdeen flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in London or Aberdeen.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Aberdeen, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
