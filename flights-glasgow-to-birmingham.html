Glasgow to Birmingham at a glance

The straight-line distance between Glasgow and Birmingham is about 252 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h to 1 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

Arriving in Birmingham

Birmingham is served by Birmingham (BHX).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Glasgow to Birmingham flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Glasgow or Birmingham.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Birmingham, especially if you arrive late at night.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Glasgow to Birmingham or a coach from Glasgow to Birmingham. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
