Tampa to Philadelphia at a glance

The straight-line distance between Tampa and Philadelphia is about 928 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 15 min to 2 h 45 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Tampa to Philadelphia flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Philadelphia, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Tampa or Philadelphia.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
