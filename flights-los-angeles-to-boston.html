Los Angeles to Boston at a glance

The straight-line distance between Los Angeles and Boston is about 2,591 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 5 h 20 min to 6 h 35 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.
