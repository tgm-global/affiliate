Tampa to Miami by train at a glance

For the roughly 331 km between Tampa and Miami, expect a journey of 4 h 05 min to 6 h 55 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Tampa: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Miami: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Miami.

Tips for Tampa to Miami train tickets

  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
