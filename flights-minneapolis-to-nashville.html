Minneapolis to Nashville at a glance

On the map, Minneapolis and Nashville are roughly 1,122 km apart. A typical flying time is 1 h 45 min to 2 h 15 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Minneapolis

Minneapolis is served by Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP).

Arriving in Nashville

Nashville is served by Nashville (BNA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Minneapolis to Nashville flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Nashville, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Minneapolis or Nashville.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
