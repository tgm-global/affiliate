Los Angeles to Houston at a glance

The straight-line distance between Los Angeles and Houston is about 1,371 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h to 3 h 45 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.
