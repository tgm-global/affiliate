Nashville to Jacksonville by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 11 h 30 min to 17 h 20 min for the 804 km between Nashville and Jacksonville. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Nashville and Jacksonville may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Jacksonville.

Tips for Nashville to Jacksonville bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Nashville and Jacksonville. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
