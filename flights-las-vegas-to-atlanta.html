Las Vegas to Atlanta at a glance

The straight-line distance between Las Vegas and Atlanta is about 1,742 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 45 min to 4 h 40 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Las Vegas

Las Vegas is served by Harry Reid (LAS).

Arriving in Atlanta

Atlanta is served by Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Las Vegas to Atlanta flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Las Vegas and Atlanta, so check each before you decide.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Las Vegas or Atlanta.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
