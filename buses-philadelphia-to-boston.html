Philadelphia to Boston by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 6 h 20 min to 9 h 40 min for the 436 km between Philadelphia and Boston. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Philadelphia and Boston may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Boston.

Tips for Philadelphia to Boston bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Philadelphia and Boston. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
