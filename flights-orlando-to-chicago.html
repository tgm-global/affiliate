Orlando to Chicago at a glance

The straight-line distance between Orlando and Chicago is about 986 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 20 min to 2 h 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Orlando to Chicago flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Chicago, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Orlando or Chicago.
