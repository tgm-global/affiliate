Leeds to Cardiff at a glance

On the map, Leeds and Cardiff are roughly 280 km apart. A typical flying time is 50 min to 1 h, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Leeds

Leeds is served by Leeds Bradford (LBA).

Arriving in Cardiff

Cardiff is served by Cardiff (CWL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Leeds to Cardiff flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Cardiff, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Leeds and Cardiff, so check each before you decide.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Leeds to Cardiff or a coach from Leeds to Cardiff. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
