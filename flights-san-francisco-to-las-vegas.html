Expect a flight of 1 h 15 min to 1 h 35 min for the 670 km between San Francisco and Las Vegas. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Tips for booking San Francisco to Las Vegas flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around San Francisco and Las Vegas, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
