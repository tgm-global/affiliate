Dallas to Nashville at a glance

The straight-line distance between Dallas and Nashville is about 616 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 40 min to 2 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Dallas

Dallas is served by Dallas Fort Worth (DFW) and Love Field (DAL).

Arriving in Nashville

Nashville is served by Nashville (BNA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Dallas to Nashville flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Nashville, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Dallas and Nashville, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Dallas or Nashville.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
