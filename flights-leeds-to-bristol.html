Leeds to Bristol at a glance

The straight-line distance between Leeds and Bristol is about 168 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 50 min to 1 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Leeds

Leeds is served by Leeds Bradford (LBA).

Arriving in Bristol

Bristol is served by Bristol (BRS).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Leeds to Bristol flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Bristol, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.

Other ways to travel
