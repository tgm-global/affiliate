Manchester to London at a glance

The straight-line distance between Manchester and London is about 162 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 50 min to 1 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Manchester

Manchester is served by Manchester (MAN).

Arriving in London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Manchester to London flights

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Manchester and London, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Manchester to London or a coach from Manchester to London. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
