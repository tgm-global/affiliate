Chicago to San Diego at a glance

The straight-line distance between Chicago and San Diego is about 1,731 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 3 h 45 min to 4 h 35 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.
