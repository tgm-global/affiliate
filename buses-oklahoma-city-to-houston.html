Oklahoma City to Houston by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 9 h 35 min to 14 h 25 min for the 666 km between Oklahoma City and Houston. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Oklahoma City and Houston may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Houston.

Tips for Oklahoma City to Houston bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Oklahoma City and Houston. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
