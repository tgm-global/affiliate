Belfast to Birmingham at a glance

On the map, Belfast and Birmingham are roughly 355 km apart. A typical flying time is 55 min to 1 h 10 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Belfast

Belfast is served by Belfast International (BFS) and George Best Belfast City (BHD).

Arriving in Birmingham

Birmingham is served by Birmingham (BHX).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Belfast to Birmingham flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Birmingham, especially if you arrive late at night.
