Houston to Fort Worth by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 5 h 35 min to 8 h 30 min for the 382 km between Houston and Fort Worth. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Houston and Fort Worth may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Fort Worth.

Tips for Houston to Fort Worth bus tickets

  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Houston and Fort Worth. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
