Boston to Charlotte at a glance

Expect a flight of 1 h 50 min to 2 h 15 min for the 1,159 km between Boston and Charlotte. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Boston

Boston is served by Logan (BOS).

Arriving in Charlotte

Charlotte is served by Charlotte Douglas (CLT).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Boston to Charlotte flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Charlotte, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Boston or Charlotte.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
