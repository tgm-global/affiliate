El Paso to Austin by bus at a glance

A bus from El Paso to Austin typically takes 12 h 05 min to 18 h 15 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

El Paso and Austin may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Austin.

Tips for El Paso to Austin bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in El Paso and Austin. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
