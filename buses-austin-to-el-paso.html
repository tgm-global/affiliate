Austin to El Paso by bus at a glance

By road, Austin to El Paso is about 528 miles, and we estimate 12 h 05 min to 18 h 15 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Austin and El Paso may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in El Paso.

Tips for Austin to El Paso bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
