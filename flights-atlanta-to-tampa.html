Atlanta to Tampa at a glance

The straight-line distance between Atlanta and Tampa is about 417 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 15 min to 1 h 35 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Atlanta

Atlanta is served by Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL).

Arriving in Tampa

Tampa is served by Tampa (TPA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Atlanta to Tampa flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Atlanta and Tampa, so check each before you decide.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Atlanta or Tampa.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
