Austin to Dallas by bus at a glance

A bus from Austin to Dallas typically takes 4 h 20 min to 6 h 40 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Austin and Dallas may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Dallas.

Tips for Austin to Dallas bus tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Austin and Dallas. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
