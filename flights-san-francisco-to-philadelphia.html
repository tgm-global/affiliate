San Francisco to Philadelphia at a glance

On the map, San Francisco and Philadelphia are roughly 4,050 km apart. A typical flying time is 5 h 10 min to 6 h 25 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Tips for booking San Francisco to Philadelphia flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around San Francisco and Philadelphia, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Philadelphia, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
