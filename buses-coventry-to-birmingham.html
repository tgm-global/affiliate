Coventry to Birmingham by coach at a glance

A coach from Coventry to Birmingham typically takes 35 min to 1 h 05 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Coventry and Birmingham may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Birmingham.

Tips for Coventry to Birmingham coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check where coaches stop in Coventry and Birmingham. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
