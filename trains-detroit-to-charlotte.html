Detroit to Charlotte by train at a glance

A train from Detroit to Charlotte typically takes 9 h 30 min to 16 h 10 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Detroit: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Charlotte: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Charlotte.

Tips for Detroit to Charlotte train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
