Chicago to Richmond by train at a glance

For the roughly 995 km between Chicago and Richmond, expect a journey of 11 h 35 min to 19 h 40 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.
