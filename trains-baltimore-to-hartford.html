Baltimore to Hartford by train at a glance

For the roughly 431 km between Baltimore and Hartford, expect a journey of 3 h 35 min to 6 h 10 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

Baltimore: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Hartford: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Hartford.

Tips for Baltimore to Hartford train tickets

  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Hartford and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Allow extra time for connections. Longer routes can run late, and a missed connection can be difficult on routes with limited service.
