Brighton to Bristol by train at a glance

For the roughly 184 km between Brighton and Bristol, expect a journey of 1 h 30 min to 2 h 45 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Brighton: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Bristol: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Bristol.

Tips for Brighton to Bristol train tickets

  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
