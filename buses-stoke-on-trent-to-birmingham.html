Stoke-on-Trent to Birmingham by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 1 h 05 min to 1 h 50 min for the 60 km between Stoke-on-Trent and Birmingham. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Stoke-on-Trent and Birmingham may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Birmingham.

Tips for Stoke-on-Trent to Birmingham coach tickets

  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check where coaches stop in Stoke-on-Trent and Birmingham. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
