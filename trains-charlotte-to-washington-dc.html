Charlotte to Washington, DC by train at a glance

For the roughly 530 km between Charlotte and Washington, DC, expect a journey of 6 h 20 min to 10 h 45 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Charlotte: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Washington, DC: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Washington, DC.

Tips for Charlotte to Washington, DC train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Washington, DC and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
