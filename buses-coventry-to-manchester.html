Coventry to Manchester by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 2 h 05 min to 3 h 25 min for the 128 km between Coventry and Manchester. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Coventry and Manchester may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Manchester.

Tips for Coventry to Manchester coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check where coaches stop in Coventry and Manchester. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
