Manchester to Miami at a glance

The straight-line distance between Manchester and Miami is about 4,321 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 8 h 45 min to 10 h 55 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Manchester

Manchester is served by Manchester (MAN).

Arriving in Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Manchester to Miami flights

  • Check US entry requirements well before you travel. Depending on your nationality you may need an ESTA or a visa.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Westbound daytime flights often suit a first day of sightseeing, while overnight flights suit people who can sleep on board.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Manchester and Miami, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Manchester or Miami.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
