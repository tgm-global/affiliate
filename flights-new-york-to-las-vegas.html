New York to Las Vegas at a glance

The straight-line distance between New York and Las Vegas is about 2,227 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 4 h 40 min to 5 h 45 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

Arriving in Las Vegas

Las Vegas is served by Harry Reid (LAS).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to Las Vegas flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Las Vegas, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in New York or Las Vegas.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
