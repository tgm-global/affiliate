Phoenix to Denver at a glance

The straight-line distance between Phoenix and Denver is about 585 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 35 min to 2 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Phoenix

Phoenix is served by Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX).

Arriving in Denver

Denver is served by Denver (DEN).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Phoenix to Denver flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Phoenix and Denver, so check each before you decide.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Phoenix or Denver.
