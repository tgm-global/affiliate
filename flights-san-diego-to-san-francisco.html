San Diego to San Francisco at a glance

The straight-line distance between San Diego and San Francisco is about 458 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 20 min to 1 h 40 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from San Diego

San Diego is served by San Diego (SAN).

Arriving in San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking San Diego to San Francisco flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in San Francisco, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from San Diego to San Francisco or a coach from San Diego to San Francisco. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
