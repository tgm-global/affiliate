Leeds to Glasgow at a glance

On the map, Leeds and Glasgow are roughly 286 km apart. A typical flying time is 50 min to 1 h, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Leeds

Leeds is served by Leeds Bradford (LBA).

Arriving in Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Leeds to Glasgow flights

  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Leeds or Glasgow.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Leeds and Glasgow, so check each before you decide.

Other ways to travel
