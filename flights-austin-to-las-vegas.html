Austin to Las Vegas at a glance

Tips for booking Austin to Las Vegas flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Austin and Las Vegas, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Las Vegas, especially if you arrive late at night.
