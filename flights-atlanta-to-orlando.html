Atlanta to Orlando at a glance

The straight-line distance between Atlanta and Orlando is about 402 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 15 min to 1 h 35 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Atlanta to Orlando flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Orlando, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Atlanta or Orlando.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Atlanta and Orlando, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
