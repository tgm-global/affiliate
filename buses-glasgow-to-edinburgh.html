Glasgow to Edinburgh by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 1 h 10 min to 2 h for the 66 km between Glasgow and Edinburgh. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Glasgow and Edinburgh may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Edinburgh.

Tips for Glasgow to Edinburgh coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Glasgow and Edinburgh. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
