What a package to Durham usually includes

Packages combine two or more parts of a trip, most often flights and a hotel, sometimes with transfers, car hire or tours. The partner's page lists what each offer includes. Check UK entry requirements before you book.

Getting there

Durham does not have a major commercial airport of its own, so most travellers fly via Newcastle (NCL). You can also see all travel options for Durham.

Tips for booking a package to Durham

  • Check what is included: flights, hotel, transfers, baggage, meals and any resort or city fees.
  • Check the location of the hotel. A lower price can mean longer transfers into the centre.
  • Book earlier for school holidays and peak seasons, when availability narrows.
  • Check passport and entry requirements for all travellers before you book.
  • Read the cancellation and change terms for the whole package, not just the hotel.
