Seattle to Las Vegas at a glance

The straight-line distance between Seattle and Las Vegas is about 872 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 05 min to 2 h 40 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Arriving in Las Vegas
