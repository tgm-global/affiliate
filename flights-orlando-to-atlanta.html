Orlando to Atlanta at a glance

The straight-line distance between Orlando and Atlanta is about 402 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 15 min to 1 h 35 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Tips for booking Orlando to Atlanta flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Orlando and Atlanta, so check each before you decide.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Orlando or Atlanta.
