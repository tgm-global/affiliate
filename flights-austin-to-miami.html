Austin to Miami at a glance

On the map, Austin and Miami are roughly 1,792 km apart. A typical flying time is 2 h 35 min to 3 h 10 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Tips for booking Austin to Miami flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Austin or Miami.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Austin and Miami, so check each before you decide.
