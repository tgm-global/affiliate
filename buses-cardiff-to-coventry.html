Cardiff to Coventry by coach at a glance

By road, Cardiff to Coventry is about 96 miles, and we estimate 2 h 30 min to 4 h on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Cardiff and Coventry may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Coventry.

Tips for Cardiff to Coventry coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Cardiff and Coventry. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
