Glasgow to Liverpool at a glance

On the map, Glasgow and Liverpool are roughly 284 km apart. A typical flying time is 50 min to 1 h, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

Arriving in Liverpool

Liverpool is served by Liverpool John Lennon (LPL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Glasgow to Liverpool flights

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Liverpool, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from Glasgow to Liverpool or a coach from Glasgow to Liverpool. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
