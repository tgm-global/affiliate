Tips for booking Las Vegas to Charlotte flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Charlotte, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Las Vegas and Charlotte, so check each before you decide.
