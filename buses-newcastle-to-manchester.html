Newcastle to Manchester by coach at a glance

By road, Newcastle to Manchester is about 107 miles, and we estimate 2 h 45 min to 4 h 20 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Newcastle and Manchester may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Manchester.

Tips for Newcastle to Manchester coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
