Nottingham to Plymouth by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 5 h 25 min to 8 h 25 min for the 353 km between Nottingham and Plymouth. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Nottingham and Plymouth may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Plymouth.

Tips for Nottingham to Plymouth coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check where coaches stop in Nottingham and Plymouth. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
