Orlando to Washington, DC at a glance

The straight-line distance between Orlando and Washington, DC is about 758 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 55 min to 2 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Orlando

Orlando is served by Orlando (MCO).

Arriving in Washington, DC

Washington, DC is served by Reagan National (DCA), Dulles (IAD) and Baltimore/Washington (BWI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Orlando to Washington, DC flights

  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Washington, DC, especially if you arrive late at night.
