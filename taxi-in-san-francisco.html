Airport transfers for San Francisco

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC). Transfer time and cost depend on the airport you use, the time of day and traffic, so check the details on your booking.

Pre-booked or on the day?

On-the-day taxis and rideshares are convenient for short trips. Pre-booking gives you a confirmed vehicle and a quoted price before you travel, which many people prefer after a long flight. Compare both and choose what suits your arrival time.

Intercity transfers from San Francisco

For longer private journeys, these are the closest places in our guide. Times are rough estimates, so confirm them on your booking.

  • San Francisco to Oakland: about 13 km, roughly 10 min to 15 min by road
  • San Francisco to San Jose: about 66 km, roughly 1 h to 1 h 20 min by road
  • San Francisco to Sacramento: about 121 km, roughly 1 h 45 min to 2 h 25 min by road

Tips for taxi and transfer bookings in San Francisco

  • Check where the meeting point is. Airports often have a set pick-up area.
  • Pre-book if you arrive late at night, with young children or with lots of luggage.
  • Check luggage space if you are travelling in a group.
  • Use licensed operators only, and check that the vehicle and driver match your booking.
  • Ask for a child seat when you book if you need one.

Frequently asked questions

Should I pre-book a taxi in San Francisco?

Pre-booking is helpful if you arrive late, travel with children or have a lot of luggage. For short daytime trips, an on-the-day taxi may be enough.

How do I get from the airport to the centre of San Francisco?

San Francisco is served by San Francisco (SFO), Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC). Options usually include taxis, transfers and public transport. Check which suits your arrival time.

Are fares fixed?

Pre-booked transfers are usually quoted in advance, while metered taxis depend on distance and time. Confirm before you travel.
