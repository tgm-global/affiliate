Chicago to Miami at a glance

The straight-line distance between Chicago and Miami is about 1,192 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 40 min to 3 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

Arriving in Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Chicago to Miami flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Chicago or Miami.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Miami, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
