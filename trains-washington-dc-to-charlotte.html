Washington, DC to Charlotte by train at a glance

For the roughly 530 km between Washington, DC and Charlotte, expect a journey of 6 h 20 min to 10 h 45 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Tips for Washington, DC to Charlotte train tickets

  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
  • Booking earlier can help on popular routes and around holidays, since lower fare levels can sell out.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Charlotte and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
