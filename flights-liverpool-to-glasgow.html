Liverpool to Glasgow at a glance

On the map, Liverpool and Glasgow are roughly 284 km apart. A typical flying time is 50 min to 1 h, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Liverpool

Liverpool is served by Liverpool John Lennon (LPL).

Arriving in Glasgow

Glasgow is served by Glasgow (GLA) and Glasgow Prestwick (PIK).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Liverpool to Glasgow flights

  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Glasgow, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
