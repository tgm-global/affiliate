Los Angeles to Chicago at a glance

Expect a flight of 3 h 45 min to 4 h 40 min for the 2,803 km between Los Angeles and Chicago. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Arriving in Chicago

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Los Angeles to Chicago flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Los Angeles or Chicago.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Los Angeles and Chicago, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
