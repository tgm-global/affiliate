Cardiff to Bradford by coach at a glance

By road, Cardiff to Bradford is about 171 miles, and we estimate 4 h 15 min to 6 h 40 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Cardiff and Bradford may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bradford.

Tips for Cardiff to Bradford coach tickets

  • Check where coaches stop in Cardiff and Bradford. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
