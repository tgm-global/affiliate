Birmingham to Leeds by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 2 h 25 min to 3 h 50 min for the 148 km between Birmingham and Leeds. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Birmingham and Leeds may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Leeds.

Tips for Birmingham to Leeds coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
