Glasgow to Stoke-on-Trent by train at a glance

For the roughly 345 km between Glasgow and Stoke-on-Trent, expect a journey of 2 h 45 min to 5 h. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Glasgow: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Stoke-on-Trent: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Stoke-on-Trent.

Tips for Glasgow to Stoke-on-Trent train tickets

  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Stoke-on-Trent. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
