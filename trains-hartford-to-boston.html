Hartford to Boston by train at a glance

For the roughly 148 km between Hartford and Boston, expect a journey of 1 h 25 min to 2 h 25 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Both cities are on the Northeast Corridor, where trains run more frequently than on most other US routes.

Stations and getting around

Hartford: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Boston: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Boston.

Tips for Hartford to Boston train tickets

  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Boston and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Check whether your train has reserved or unreserved coach seating, because this varies by route.
