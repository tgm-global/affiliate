Charlotte to Nashville by bus at a glance

A bus from Charlotte to Nashville typically takes 7 h 55 min to 11 h 55 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Charlotte and Nashville may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Nashville.

Tips for Charlotte to Nashville bus tickets

  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
