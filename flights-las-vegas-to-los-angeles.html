Tips for booking Las Vegas to Los Angeles flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Los Angeles, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Las Vegas and Los Angeles, so check each before you decide.

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Las Vegas or Los Angeles.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
