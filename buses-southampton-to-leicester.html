Southampton to Leicester by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 3 h 05 min to 4 h 50 min for the 194 km between Southampton and Leicester. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Southampton and Leicester may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Leicester.

Tips for Southampton to Leicester coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Check where coaches stop in Southampton and Leicester. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
