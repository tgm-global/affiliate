Birmingham to Edinburgh at a glance

On the map, Birmingham and Edinburgh are roughly 394 km apart. A typical flying time is 55 min to 1 h 10 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Birmingham to Edinburgh flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Birmingham or Edinburgh.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Edinburgh, especially if you arrive late at night.
