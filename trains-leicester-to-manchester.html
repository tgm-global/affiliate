Leicester to Manchester by train at a glance

For the roughly 119 km between Leicester and Manchester, expect a journey of 1 h to 1 h 50 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Leicester: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Manchester: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Manchester.

Tips for Leicester to Manchester train tickets

  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Advance tickets are tied to a specific train and are often cheaper, while off-peak and anytime tickets are more flexible. Check the rules before you buy.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Manchester. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
