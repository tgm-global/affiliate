Nashville to New Orleans at a glance

The straight-line distance between Nashville and New Orleans is about 469 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 20 min to 1 h 40 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in New Orleans, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Nashville or New Orleans.
