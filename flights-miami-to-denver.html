Miami to Denver at a glance

On the map, Miami and Denver are roughly 2,776 km apart. A typical flying time is 3 h 40 min to 4 h 35 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

Arriving in Denver

Denver is served by Denver (DEN).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Miami to Denver flights

  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Denver, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
