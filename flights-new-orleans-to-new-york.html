New Orleans to New York at a glance

The straight-line distance between New Orleans and New York is about 1,168 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 40 min to 3 h 20 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from New Orleans

New Orleans is served by Louis Armstrong New Orleans (MSY).

Arriving in New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New Orleans to New York flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in New York, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around New Orleans and New York, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
