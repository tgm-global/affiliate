Nottingham to Hull by train at a glance

For the roughly 102 km between Nottingham and Hull, expect a journey of 55 min to 1 h 40 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Nottingham: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Hull: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Hull.

Tips for Nottingham to Hull train tickets

  • Look at which station you will arrive at in Hull. Large cities can have several, and the best one depends on where you are staying.
  • Keep your ticket or digital ticket available until you leave the arrival station, since gates and inspections can require it.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
