New York to Charlotte at a glance

Expect a flight of 1 h 30 min to 1 h 50 min for the 853 km between New York and Charlotte. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from New York

New York is served by John F. Kennedy (JFK), LaGuardia (LGA) and Newark (EWR).

Arriving in Charlotte

Charlotte is served by Charlotte Douglas (CLT).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking New York to Charlotte flights

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from New York to Charlotte or a coach from New York to Charlotte. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
