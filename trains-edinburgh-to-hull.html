Edinburgh to Hull by train at a glance

A train from Edinburgh to Hull typically takes 2 h 25 min to 4 h 25 min. These figures are estimates based on distance, so always confirm the timetable for your date. Great Britain has a dense rail network, so many pairs of large cities have frequent services, though some need a change of train.

Stations and getting around

Edinburgh: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. Hull: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in Hull.

Tips for Edinburgh to Hull train tickets

  • Reserve a seat on busy long-distance trains, particularly on Fridays, Sundays and around holidays.
  • Splitting a journey into separate tickets can sometimes lower the cost. Read the conditions carefully because you must stay on the same train.
  • Railcards can reduce fares for eligible travellers, so check whether one applies to you before you book.
  • Check for planned engineering work, which is more common at weekends and can add time or require a replacement bus.
  • Travelling off-peak is usually quieter and can be cheaper than commuter hours on weekdays.
