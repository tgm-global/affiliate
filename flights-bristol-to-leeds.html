Bristol to Leeds at a glance

The straight-line distance between Bristol and Leeds is about 168 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 50 min to 1 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Bristol

Bristol is served by Bristol (BRS).

Arriving in Leeds

Leeds is served by Leeds Bradford (LBA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Bristol to Leeds flights

  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Bristol or Leeds.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Leeds, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.

Other ways to travel
