Coventry to Cardiff by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 2 h 30 min to 4 h for the 154 km between Coventry and Cardiff. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Coventry and Cardiff may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Cardiff.

Tips for Coventry to Cardiff coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check where coaches stop in Coventry and Cardiff. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
