Miami to Tampa at a glance

Expect a flight of 55 min to 1 h 05 min for the 331 km between Miami and Tampa. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

Arriving in Tampa

Tampa is served by Tampa (TPA).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Miami to Tampa flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Tampa, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Miami and Tampa, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
