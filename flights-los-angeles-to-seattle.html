Los Angeles to Seattle at a glance

On the map, Los Angeles and Seattle are roughly 1,545 km apart. A typical flying time is 2 h 15 min to 2 h 50 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Arriving in Seattle

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Los Angeles to Seattle flights

  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Seattle, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Los Angeles or Seattle.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
