Detroit to Indianapolis by bus at a glance

By road, Detroit to Indianapolis is about 240 miles, and we estimate 5 h 40 min to 8 h 35 min on a bus. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Detroit and Indianapolis may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Indianapolis.

Tips for Detroit to Indianapolis bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
