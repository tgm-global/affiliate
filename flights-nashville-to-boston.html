Nashville to Boston at a glance

The straight-line distance between Nashville and Boston is about 942 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 15 min to 2 h 50 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Nashville

Nashville is served by Nashville (BNA).

Arriving in Boston

Boston is served by Logan (BOS).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Nashville to Boston flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Nashville and Boston, so check each before you decide.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Nashville or Boston.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
