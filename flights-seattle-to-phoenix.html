Seattle to Phoenix at a glance

The straight-line distance between Seattle and Phoenix is about 1,115 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 35 min to 3 h 10 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Seattle

Seattle is served by Seattle-Tacoma (SEA).

Arriving in Phoenix

Phoenix is served by Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Seattle to Phoenix flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Seattle and Phoenix, so check each before you decide.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Phoenix, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Seattle or Phoenix.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
