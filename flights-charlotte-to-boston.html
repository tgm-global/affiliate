Charlotte to Boston at a glance

The straight-line distance between Charlotte and Boston is about 720 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 1 h 50 min to 2 h 15 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Charlotte

Charlotte is served by Charlotte Douglas (CLT).

Arriving in Boston

Boston is served by Logan (BOS).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Charlotte to Boston flights

  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Charlotte and Boston, so check each before you decide.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
