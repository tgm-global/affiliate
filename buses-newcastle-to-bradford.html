Newcastle to Bradford by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 2 h 10 min to 3 h 30 min for the 131 km between Newcastle and Bradford. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Newcastle and Bradford may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bradford.

Tips for Newcastle to Bradford coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check where coaches stop in Newcastle and Bradford. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
