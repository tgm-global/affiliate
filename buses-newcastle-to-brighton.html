Newcastle to Brighton by coach at a glance

By road, Newcastle to Brighton is about 294 miles, and we estimate 7 h 10 min to 11 h 10 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Newcastle and Brighton may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Brighton.

Tips for Newcastle to Brighton coach tickets

  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
  • Check where coaches stop in Newcastle and Brighton. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
