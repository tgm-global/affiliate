Newcastle to Glasgow by coach at a glance

By road, Newcastle to Glasgow is about 120 miles, and we estimate 3 h to 4 h 50 min on a coach. Traffic, rest stops and the number of pick-ups all affect the real time.

Stops and arrival

Newcastle and Glasgow may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Glasgow.

Tips for Newcastle to Glasgow coach tickets

  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check where coaches stop in Newcastle and Glasgow. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
