Miami to Chicago at a glance

On the map, Miami and Chicago are roughly 1,917 km apart. A typical flying time is 2 h 40 min to 3 h 20 min, but the total journey depends on how early you arrive, which airports you use and whether your flight is direct.

Airports for this route

Departing from Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

Arriving in Chicago

Chicago is served by O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Miami to Chicago flights

  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Miami and Chicago, so check each before you decide.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Chicago, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
