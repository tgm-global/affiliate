Hull to Edinburgh by coach at a glance

Expect roughly 4 h 40 min to 7 h 25 min for the 306 km between Hull and Edinburgh. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Hull and Edinburgh may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Edinburgh.

Tips for Hull to Edinburgh coach tickets

  • Look for services with comfort features you need, such as power sockets, Wi-Fi or space for wheelchairs, because these vary by operator.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check where coaches stop in Hull and Edinburgh. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
  • Book earlier for weekends, school holidays and events, since lower fares can sell out.
