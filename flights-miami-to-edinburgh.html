Miami to Edinburgh at a glance

Expect a flight of 7 h 45 min to 9 h 35 min for the 6,864 km between Miami and Edinburgh. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from Miami

Miami is served by Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).

Arriving in Edinburgh

Edinburgh is served by Edinburgh (EDI).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Miami to Edinburgh flights

  • Check UK entry requirements before you travel. Many visitors need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or a visa, depending on nationality.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Overnight eastbound flights usually land early in the morning, so think about how you will spend your first day.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Miami and Edinburgh, so check each before you decide.
  • Midweek and off-peak departures are often quieter than Fridays and Sundays, although prices vary with season and demand.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Edinburgh, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
