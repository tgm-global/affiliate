Birmingham to Atlanta at a glance

The straight-line distance between Birmingham and Atlanta is about 4,114 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 8 h 25 min to 10 h 25 min, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Birmingham

Birmingham is served by Birmingham (BHX).

Arriving in Atlanta

Atlanta is served by Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Birmingham to Atlanta flights

  • Check US entry requirements well before you travel. Depending on your nationality you may need an ESTA or a visa.
  • Check that your passport is valid for the whole stay, and read the airline's rules on documents for transatlantic flights.
  • Plan for jet lag. Westbound daytime flights often suit a first day of sightseeing, while overnight flights suit people who can sleep on board.
  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in Birmingham or Atlanta.
  • Check the full journey time, including airport transfers and security, before you decide that flying beats other options.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Birmingham and Atlanta, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
