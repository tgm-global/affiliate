Los Angeles to San Diego by train at a glance

For the roughly 178 km between Los Angeles and San Diego, expect a journey of 2 h 20 min to 3 h 55 min. Faster services stop less, and a journey with a change takes longer. This is a US route, so frequency can be much lower than in Great Britain. Check the timetable before you plan around a specific departure.

Stations and getting around

Los Angeles: large places can have more than one station, so check which one your booking uses. San Diego: plan how you will get from the arrival station to your final stop, whether by foot, local transport or taxi in San Diego.

Tips for Los Angeles to San Diego train tickets

  • Look at which station you will arrive at in San Diego and how you will reach your hotel or meeting from there.
  • Check luggage allowances and carry-on rules before you travel, and label your bags.
  • Compare coach seats with business or sleeper options on overnight services if comfort matters more than cost.
  • Check whether a coach connection is included for part of the journey, as some rail itineraries use a bus link.
  • Corridor routes with frequent departures are easier to plan than long-distance routes that may run once a day, so check the timetable first.
