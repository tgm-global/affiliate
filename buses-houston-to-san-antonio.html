Houston to San Antonio by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 4 h 30 min to 6 h 55 min for the 304 km between Houston and San Antonio. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

Houston and San Antonio may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in San Antonio.

Tips for Houston to San Antonio bus tickets

  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight trips can save a hotel night but you arrive tired.
  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in Houston and San Antonio. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
