Seattle to Denver at a glance

The straight-line distance between Seattle and Denver is about 1,020 miles. Allowing for taxiing, climbing and approach, airlines usually schedule a flight of 2 h 25 min to 3 h, although the time on your ticket can be longer if you connect.

Airports for this route

Departing from Seattle

Seattle is served by Seattle-Tacoma (SEA).

Arriving in Denver

Denver is served by Denver (DEN).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking Seattle to Denver flights

  • Make sure the name on your booking matches your photo ID or passport exactly.
  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Check the baggage rules. Hand-luggage-only fares are common on shorter routes, and checked bags can cost extra.
  • Compare nearby airports. Fares and ground-transport time can differ between the airports around Seattle and Denver, so check each before you decide.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.
