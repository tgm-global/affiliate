London to Manchester at a glance

Expect a flight of 50 min to 1 h for the 261 km between London and Manchester. Your door-to-door time is longer once you add travel to the airport, check-in, security and the journey from the arrival airport.

Airports for this route

Departing from London

London is served by Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), Luton (LTN) and London City (LCY).

Arriving in Manchester

Manchester is served by Manchester (MAN).

When prices look similar, choose the airport that makes your onward journey simplest. Check transfer time as well as the fare.

Tips for booking London to Manchester flights

  • Compare a return fare with two one-way fares. The best option depends on your plans.
  • Turn on fare or schedule alerts with your booking partner so you hear about changes early.
  • Book earlier for school holidays, public holidays and major events in London or Manchester.
  • Allow time for the journey between the airport and your final stop in Manchester, especially if you arrive late at night.
  • Read the fare conditions for changes and refunds before you pay. Lower fares are usually less flexible.

Other ways to travel

If you would rather stay on the ground, compare a train from London to Manchester or a coach from London to Manchester. Which is best depends on your budget, luggage and how much time you have.
