New York to Washington, DC by bus at a glance

Expect roughly 4 h 50 min to 7 h 25 min for the 327 km between New York and Washington, DC. Express services with few stops are faster, and busy periods on the road can add time.

Stops and arrival

New York and Washington, DC may each have more than one place where buses stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Washington, DC.

Tips for New York to Washington, DC bus tickets

  • Allow for traffic and weather. Intercity journey times can change at busy times and on longer routes.
  • Look for services with the comfort features you need, such as power sockets or Wi-Fi, because these vary by operator.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for additional or oversized bags.
  • Check exactly where the bus departs and arrives in New York and Washington, DC. Some intercity services use curbside stops rather than a central terminal.
  • Book earlier for holiday weekends and events, since lower fares can sell out.
