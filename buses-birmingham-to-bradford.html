Birmingham to Bradford by coach at a glance

A coach from Birmingham to Bradford typically takes 2 h 20 min to 3 h 50 min. We base this on distance, so use the partner search for the actual timetable on your date.

Stops and arrival

Birmingham and Bradford may each have more than one place where coaches stop, and these are not always next to the main railway station. Check the exact pick-up and drop-off point on your booking, and plan the last part of your journey, for example with a taxi or transfer in Bradford.

Tips for Birmingham to Bradford coach tickets

  • Arrive at the stop early and keep your ticket or digital ticket ready to show the driver.
  • Compare daytime and overnight services. Overnight journeys can be cheaper but you arrive tired and early.
  • Check where coaches stop in Birmingham and Bradford. The coach station is often not the same place as the main railway station.
  • Allow for traffic. Coach journey times can change at busy times, especially on roads into large cities.
  • Check luggage rules and any extra charge for large bags or oversized items.
